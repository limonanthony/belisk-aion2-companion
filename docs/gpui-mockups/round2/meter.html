Meter fixes proposals on a copy of the approved meter

  • Header under the pointer: seven buttons leave the boss name a few letters ("Veilwarden Ors…"). Options: a, a second row of buttons below the header; b, three buttons and a menu for the rest (shown open); c, the buttons take the boss gauge's line while the pointer is over the card (recommended).
  • Combat power beside names is hard to read over the bright scene. Options: a, approved (0.66); b, 0.82 (recommended); c, 0.82 and a little larger and heavier.
  • Everything else is the approved meter. Compare with the "Live, pointer over" and "Live, locked" tiles over the bright scene.

What each header option costs

  • a, second row: every control stays one click away and the name keeps the full width, but the window grows by 26 px under the pointer. With auto-resize on, a window that grows on hover can push its edge under the cursor.
  • b, overflow menu: the header keeps its height and the name most of its width, but Reset, History, Settings and PvP take two clicks. The menu is a popover inside the overlay window, so the window has to be tall enough to hold it (GPUI cannot draw outside its window).
  • c, buttons replace the gauge line (recommended): same height (the gauge block is fixed at 22 px in every state, a few pixels taller than approved), all seven controls one click away, the name gets its full width plus the fight time. Under the pointer you do not see the gauge; it comes back when the pointer leaves.

GPUI notes

  • All three are plain divs, buttons and one popover; nothing new for GPUI. Hover swaps the content; there is no animation beyond a 100 ms cross-fade.
  • Combat power 0.82 on the warm near-black stays clear of the player name (0.96), so the hierarchy holds.
