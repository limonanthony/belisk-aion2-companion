Meter overlay round 4: toolbar, open-world view, not identified yet

  • Toolbar D (your layout, recommended, the default): the target's name on top left with the full width, bright on the Target board and faint on the Session board; the time at top right, replaced by reset, capsule, lock, Copy and close under the pointer. A footer that is always there: totals on the left; ping and a fixed-width Session | Target switch on the right.
  • A, B and C, the first round 4 options, stay for comparison: they put a view label in the header instead of a switch. The Toolbar switch moves between them. No option uses the gauge line or resizes the window on hover, and locked shows no controls.
  • The Session board keeps rows stable across mobs: damage since the last reset, session DPS, share of session damage. No contribution, since there is no single target HP. All names and figures are invented.

Toolbar options

  • D, name on top, tools top right, footer switch (recommended). The header names what is being hit: bright on the Target board, faint on Session (the mob being hit right now). Under the pointer, reset, capsule, lock, Copy and close replace the time, so the window actions sit on top; the name gives up about 130 px for them and cuts off with an ellipsis. The footer is always there: Damage (party damage on this target, or session damage with the number of targets) on the left; ping and the Session | Target switch (100 px) on the right. The switch follows the automatic choice; clicking the other side pins it, and a small dot on the active side shows the pin. Clicking the active side again returns to automatic. Reset and the next boss also clear the pin. When locked, the switch is still visible but does not react. History, PvP and Settings will go behind one "…" button before close (switch "Later controls" to "shown faint").
  • A, footer strip. Under the pointer the footer's 20 px line holds reset, capsule, lock, Copy, ping and close; at rest it shows the totals again. The header (label and time), the gauge and the rows never change under the pointer, and the approved meter already swapped the footer on hover, so the mechanic is the familiar one. Cost: close sits bottom right, and the footer totals (Party, pace) hide while the pointer is over the card, as they did before. Later controls (History, PvP, Settings) fit after lock; ping would give way if all three arrive.
  • B, header right. Under the pointer the fight time gives way to five 22 px buttons, like a title bar. Everything is one click away and the footer never changes. Cost: the label loses about 130 px exactly when you might click it (long boss names cut off), the time hides, and the label and the buttons are two different kinds of click side by side. With the later controls the label drops to about 90 px.
  • C, lock + menu. Under the pointer only lock and a menu button join the time. The card changes the least, but reset, capsule, copy and close take two clicks, and the menu must fit inside the card: GPUI cannot draw outside the window, and with one row or the waiting card it does not fit without growing the window.

Decided for D

  • Session: damage in bold, sorted by damage; DPS is the quiet figure, with a faint "/s". Session DPS and the header time count combat time.
  • A pin lasts until reset or the next boss. A pinned Target board follows your current target.
  • "Not identified yet": the hint gets its own 20 px line between the rows and the footer, because the always-on footer has no room left for it; the line goes away once the game names you.
  • Waiting: no switch, since there is no board yet. The footer shows ping, CPU and memory, as approved.

Open choices for A–C

  • Current target in Session: a, nothing; b, the target's name after the label, faint (recommended), which costs no height and names what a click on the label would show; c, a line under the header with the target and a short gauge, so both views keep the same skeleton, at 22 px of height for trash that dies in seconds.
  • Session figure: damage, sorted by damage (recommended), so a passer-by with two big hits does not outrank the party; or DPS, sorted by DPS, laid out like the boss board. The quiet figure carries a faint "/s" when it is DPS, because the two boards put different figures in bold.
  • Later controls (all options): left out (recommended; only working controls ship) or shown faint where they would go.

Intended motion

  • Pointer over: the tools cross-fade with the time in 100 ms and back when the pointer leaves (the approved hover swap). The window keeps its size.
  • View switch (automatic, pin, unpin): the board cross-fades in 150 ms; rows trade places over 240 ms as approved. The card's height follows the new board (the Target board adds the 22 px gauge line), like any row-count change; it never changes on hover. D's switch moves its highlight across in 120 ms.
  • The tooltip over the switch (D) or under the label (A–C) appears after GPUI's usual tooltip delay and fades in over 150 ms.

GPUI notes

  • D's switch is two divs in a rounded track (the segmented-control tokens, scaled to the 20 px footer), each with a hover fill and an on_click; the pin dot is a 4 px rounded_full div. Locked, the window is click-through, so hover never fires; the view draws no hover styles anyway.
  • A–C's label is a div with rounded, a hover fill and an on_click; the pin, pin-off and swap glyphs are Lucide pin, pin-off and arrow-left-right as single-colour SVGs.
  • Tooltips and C's menu are drawn inside the overlay window. D's tooltip sits above the footer and needs about two rows; with one row it has to sit beside the switch, which leaves almost no room. C's menu cannot fit with few rows.
  • No card shadow (tokens.md). The tooltip and menu keep the popover shadow because they sit inside the card. No blur, no text shadow, two-stop gradients only; class icons are the PNGs.
