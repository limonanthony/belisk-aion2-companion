Behaviour

  • Checks Agreed: at launch, then once an hour while the app runs, never more often (GitHub's 60 requests an hour per address). Up to date, checking, offline or limited: no icon.
  • Clicks Proposed: available → the download starts at once (no confirmation); downloading, paused or checking → opens Settings › About, where Cancel and the details are; ready → restarts at once, no confirmation, as quit has none.
  • Download Agreed: always speed-capped, and paused while you fight ("On by default but download is always throttled not only during fights"). Proposed the cap is 1 MB/s, and the same rules apply when you clicked Update yourself.
  • Restart Agreed: installing always waits for your click; the app closes and starts the new version. Proposed during a fight the click still works and the tooltip warns that it splits the fight in History; the tools that were open open again in their places; then What's new opens once under the toolbar (whatsnew.html).
  • Pointer: the slot lights on pointer enter and goes on leave, never on moves inside; the progress line changes only when the download progresses (one frame per percent), never pulses.
  • Accessibility: the button's name is the tooltip's first line ("Update available: 1.6.0", "Downloading 1.6.0 · 62%", "Restart to update to 1.6.0"); the line is a progressbar with its value; Left and Right reach it like the other tools.
