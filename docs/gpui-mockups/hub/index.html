Kisk as a multi-tool: the hub one mockup round, 2026-10-08, not approved yet

  • Kisk becomes a set of tools over the game, not only a DPS meter. At launch only a small floating toolbar opens; every tool (DPS meter, PvP meter, to-dos, fight history, reminders, settings) opens and closes from it, and it shows Odyle Energy and Shugo Festival keys, with a warning when other resources are full.

Behaviour annotated, not drawn

  • Agreed At launch only the toolbar opens (the meter too when Settings › Overlay › "Open the meter at launch" is on).
  • Agreed Capture always runs. Fights (damage, heals, damage taken, deaths, fight history) are tracked only while the meter is open. Resources always stay up to date when the game sends them.
  • Agreed Alt+CapsLock keeps locking the meter. Alt+Shift+CapsLock hides the toolbar and every open tool, and shows them again; the tray icon's "Show toolbar" too.
  • Agreed Closing the toolbar quits Kisk (the process ends). The tray icon's menu: Show toolbar, Quit.
  • Agreed The meter's "…" holds only the boss setting; History, To-dos, Settings and PvP are in the toolbar.
  • Proposed The toolbar, its dropdowns and tooltips never take the keyboard from the game, like the reminder card. Settings stays a normal window.
