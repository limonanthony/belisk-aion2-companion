The toolbar hub round, 2026-10-08, not approved yet

  • The hub of Kisk: a small horizontal bar floating over the game. It opens alone at launch; every tool opens and closes from it. Like the reminder card it never takes the keyboard from the game (no-activate, tool window), and it is never click-through: Alt+CapsLock locks the meter only.
  • Left to right: the grip; DPS meter (crossed swords), PvP meter, To-dos, Fight history (crown), Reminders (bell), Settings; Odyle Energy and Shugo Festival keys; the warning when other resources are full; fold and quit. A lit tool (hot fill and a dot) is open; a click opens or closes it.
  • Tags: Agreed (your words, quoted in README.md), Proposed (for you to accept or change), Open (for you to decide). The Counts selector switches between icons only and icons with names. Values and times are invented; the clock is Monday 21:48.

Behaviour

  • Agreed Horizontal only, because of the counts. At launch only the toolbar opens; the meter opens from its icon (or at launch, with Settings, Overlay, "Open the meter at launch").
  • Agreed Counts: Odyle Energy and Shugo Festival keys always shown, as the to-do window draws them: the regular pool against its cap ("7/21"), the extra pool beside it when not zero ("+3"); above the cap is normal. Amber when the regular pool is full and a refill is coming. A click on either opens the Resources window.
  • Agreed The warning ("2" with Lucide triangle-alert) shows only while other resources are full and a refill is coming; a click opens the list of them, ending with "All resources", which opens the Resources window.
  • Agreed Hide everything: Alt+Shift+CapsLock hides the toolbar and every open tool, and shows them again; the tray icon's "Show toolbar" too. Alt+CapsLock keeps locking the meter.
  • Agreed Quit: closing the toolbar quits Kisk (closing the main window ends the process).
  • Proposed Focus: the toolbar, its tooltips and dropdowns never take the keyboard. Its dropdowns are drawn inside its window, which grows downward while one is open (upward when the toolbar sits low on the screen), as the meter's "…" menu does. A dropdown closes on a click elsewhere in the toolbar, a second click on its button, or the pointer leaving the toolbar and the dropdown.
  • Proposed Pointer: tools and counts light on pointer enter and go on leave, never on moves inside; tooltips after 500 ms, as the meter's.
  • Proposed Place: the toolbar opens where you left it (top centre the first time), dragged by the grip or any empty part of the bar. Tools keep their own places.
  • Proposed Fold (« at the end) leaves a small handle: the grip, unfold (») and, when anything is full (Odyle and Shugo keys included), the warning with that number.
  • Proposed Quit (× at the end, after fold) quits at once, with the tooltip "Quit Kisk / Closes every tool"; no confirmation.
  • Proposed The meter's tooltip says what closing it means: "Fights are tracked while it is open." Fights (damage, heals, damage taken, deaths, history) are tracked only while the meter is open; capture always runs and resources always stay up to date.
  • Open Reminders (bell): there is no reminders window. Drawn: the bell opens a small list of the next reminders, "Pause reminders" (the bell turns to Lucide bell-off) and "Reminder settings". Alternative: the bell opens Settings, Reminders.
  • Open PvP icon: the crossed swords are the DPS meter's now, so PvP takes Lucide crosshair (its window's header would change to match). Say if you prefer another.
  • Proposed The reminder card sits above the meter while the meter is open (approved); while it is closed, 6 px under the toolbar, left edges aligned (above it when the toolbar is low on the screen).
