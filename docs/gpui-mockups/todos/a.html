A, tabs, compact checklist earlier exploration, kept for reference: see D

  • A 340 px overlay window like PvP and History: drag it by the title, × closes it. Opened from the meter's "…" menu (To-dos).
  • Daily | Weekly tabs; the reset countdown sits right under the tabs. One line per to-do; a click on the row ticks it or adds one to its count. Order never changes, so rows do not move under the pointer.
  • Recharges (keys, Odyle Energy, dungeon entries that bank up to a cap): the meter cannot read them, so you click Used up after spending them and it counts from empty: "Full in 2 d 16 h". Once the estimate reaches the cap the row turns yellow: "Full since 09:00".
  • Manual ticks only. No characters: one list (whichever character you play). Limits are from the reset research; counts, times and names are invented. Clock: Monday 21:48.

Behaviour

  • Ticking. A click on a single to-do ticks it; on a counted one (Duty Missions 3/5) it adds one, and the row ticks itself at the last. Under the pointer a "−" takes one back. The window never takes focus from the game (the overlays' no-activate style).
  • Used up. Under the pointer, a recharge's status swaps for "Used up" in the same place (no jump). One click sets the estimate to empty now; the tooltip says when it will be full again.
  • Reset. At the daily reset the Daily tab clears; on Wednesday the Weekly tab clears. Recharges are never cleared: they only fill. The 09:00 hour is reported by a fan site, so the footer says so; a Settings row would change it.
  • Keyboard and screen readers (for the build): each row is a checkbox ("Duty Missions, 3 of 5", mixed until done); recharges are buttons ("Odyle Energy, full in 2 d 16 h. Mark used up"); Tab moves row to row, Space ticks. The ring is the approved focus-ring colour, 2 px, 1 px outside the row.
  • Edit. The pencil turns the list into show/hide toggles; hidden rows stay listed, faded, so they can come back. "Add a to-do" adds a single tick of your own, daily or weekly, with a delete button. Typing there is the only time the window takes the keyboard, like the PvP name field.
