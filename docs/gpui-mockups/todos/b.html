B, one list per character, with suggestions earlier exploration, kept for reference: see D

  • A 370 px overlay window (History's width). Under the title, the character the list belongs to: the one you are playing, picked by the meter from the characters it remembers; click to look at another.
  • One list in three sections: Today, Recharges, This week. Each section label carries its own countdown and folds away with a click. Two lines per row: the name, then one line of fact ("3 a week · solo").
  • Suggested ticks: when the meter sees a known dungeon's last boss fall, the row turns green and asks "Cleared at 21:32?". Nothing is counted until you click Count it; a count that came from the meter keeps a small mark.
  • Recharges as in A: Used up, then "Full in …" from the estimate; yellow once full. Duty Missions are shared by the characters of one account on one server, so their row says "Account". Limits are from the reset research; counts, times and names are invented. Clock: Monday 21:48.

Behaviour

  • Character. The list follows the character you play, as the meter knows it (name, server, class). Clicking the selector shows the others with what they have left; picking one shows its list without changing anything in the game. Before the game has named you, the selector says "You" and the list is the last character's.
  • Account to-dos. Duty Missions are per account and server (research): ticking them on Mirelle ticks them on Tavrin (same server), not on Sefa (Lyss). Everything else is per character [inference from the research, to check in game].
  • Suggestions. Only for weekly to-dos the meter can see end: Ascension Trial, Subjugation, the Sanctuary raids and the boss-kill counts. The suggestion stays until you answer or the week resets; × dismisses it. It never counts by itself, because a kill does not prove the reward was taken.
  • Sections. A folded section keeps its label, count and countdown, so a done section can stay folded. Folding is remembered.
  • Ticking, Used up, edit mode, keyboard roles and the focus ring: as in A. In edit mode hidden to-dos are per character (an alt may skip a raid).
