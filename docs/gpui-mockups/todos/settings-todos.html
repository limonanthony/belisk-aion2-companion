Settings, To-dos approved 2026-10-06, kept as drawn for history; the approved version is the To-dos section of approved/settings.html

  • A new To-dos section in the sidebar, after Reminders, in the approved Settings rows, switches, buttons and dialog. Changes apply at once; there is no Save.
  • Resets: the daily reset hour, 09:00 by default, with the note "Reported, not confirmed. Check the countdown in game."; the weekly reset is shown, not set: Wednesday at the same hour.
  • Membership: Odyle Energy fills up to 840 instead of 560. It left the to-do window with its edit mode.
  • Bringing the defaults back, two ways (the user's request): Restore default to-dos brings back every built-in to-do you hid and keeps your own to-dos and all progress; Reset to-dos completely also removes your own to-dos and empties every count and amount, after a confirm dialog. Both apply to all characters.
  • Character names and figures are invented.

Behaviour

  • Daily reset: a select with every hour, 00:00 to 23:00; it opens scrolled to the chosen hour. The hour moves Today's countdown, the daily recharges' estimates and the weekly reset with it. The countdowns in the to-do window follow at once. "Change" in the window's footer opens this section.
  • Weekly reset: not a control. The research says Wednesday; it follows the daily hour ("Wednesday 09:00", "Wednesday 17:00").
  • Membership: one switch for every character, off by default. On, Odyle Energy's cap is 840: an amount you typed above 560 is kept, and its "Full in …" estimate uses the new cap. Off again, an amount above 560 is cut to 560.
  • Restore default to-dos: on every character, every built-in to-do you hid comes back (Duty Commands too, which start hidden). Your own to-dos, every count and every amount stay. The description says how many are hidden now ("6 hidden, on 2 characters"); with none hidden it says "Nothing is hidden." and the button is disabled. After a click, a good badge "6 to-dos back" shows beside the button and fades after 4 s, as "Shown above the meter" does in Reminders. No dialog: nothing is lost, and anything can be hidden again from the window.
  • Reset to-dos completely: opens the dialog. Cancel has the focus, so Enter or Esc changes nothing. "Reset to-dos" does, on every character: your own to-dos are removed, every hidden to-do comes back, every count goes to 0 and every amount you typed goes back to empty ("—"). The reset hour and the membership stay. Afterwards a good badge "To-dos reset" shows beside the button for 4 s; the to-do window, if open, shows the fresh list at once.
  • Not here: the per-row hide and show, adding your own and the counts themselves stay in the to-do window. The "+1?" from boss kills (off by default, built later) would get a switch in this section when it is built.
