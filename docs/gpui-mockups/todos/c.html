C, all characters at once, with counters earlier exploration, kept for reference: see D

  • A 520 px overlay window (the player detail's width) for players with alts: one row per to-do, one column per character the meter remembers (up to three shown; the one in game has the cream rim). Daily | Weekly tabs with the countdown beside them.
  • Recharges as counters: each cell is the meter's estimate ("8/14"): it adds the refill itself at each reset or 16:00, and you correct it in a small popover after a run. A full cell turns yellow, so an alt that is wasting keys shows at a glance.
  • Account to-dos (Duty Missions) span the characters of one server. Manual ticks only; a dash means that to-do is hidden for that character.
  • Limits are from the reset research; counts, times and names are invented. Clock: Monday 21:48.

Behaviour

  • Cells. A single to-do is a tick box: one click ticks it. A count or a recharge opens the counter popover (−, +, Used all); the popover is drawn inside the window, which grows while it is open, like the meter's "…" menu. A click elsewhere closes it.
  • Counters. The meter adds the refill on its schedule (+2 at the daily reset, +4 keys at 16:00, +15 Odyle Energy every 3 h) and stops at the cap. It cannot see what you spend, so the figure is only as good as your last correction; the popover says when it will be full.
  • More than three characters. The three most recently played are shown; edit mode chooses which. The window does not grow sideways.
  • Keyboard and screen readers (for the build): the grid is a table with row and column headers; each cell is a checkbox or a button ("Nightmare, Tavrin, 10 of 14"); arrow keys move between cells. The ring: the approved focus-ring colour, 2 px, 1 px outside the cell.
