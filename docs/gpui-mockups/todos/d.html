D, you set the counts proposal, not approved: the model you chose, drawn on B with A's rows

  • The meter cannot read any of these counts from the game, so everything is set by you, in three kinds of rows: a tick (done or not), a count "9/14" with one-less and one-more arrows, and an amount for the recharges that bank up to a cap, which stays empty ("—") and ignored unless you type what you have.
  • B's window (370 px, one list per character, the selector follows the character you play) with A's one-line rows. Three folding sections, Today, Recharges and This week, each with its own countdown. Sanctuary and Exploration fold into one row each.
  • The reset hour is a setting, shown in the footer as "Daily reset 09:00 · Change": 09:00 is reported by a fan site and 17:00 has also been seen, so neither is printed as fact. The countdowns follow the setting.
  • Try it (last tile): the arrows, the counts, the ticks, the sections and groups, typing an amount, clearing it, and edit mode all work. Nothing is saved. Limits are from the reset research; counts, times and names are invented. Clock: Monday 21:48 (the fresh week: Wednesday 09:12).

Behaviour

  • Counts. One more and one less are arrow buttons beside the figure; a click on the figure itself also adds one (the largest target, for the usual "did one more"). The figure stops at 0 and at the limit, and the arrow that cannot go further dims. At the limit the box on the left gets the cream check and the row dims; rows never move. Counts in Today go back to 0 at the daily reset, counts in This week on Wednesday.
  • Ticks. A click anywhere on the row ticks or unticks it (Abyssal Forge's final boss, a to-do of your own).
  • Amounts. Empty by default: a dash, the name in muted, no estimate, no warning, not counted anywhere. A click on the dash opens a field: type what the game shows now, Enter keeps it, Esc cancels, a click elsewhere keeps it. From then on the meter adds the refill on schedule (+15 Odyle Energy every 3 h, +4 Shugo keys at 16:00, +2 at the daily reset, …) and says "Full in 1 d 2 h"; at the cap the row turns yellow ("Full, being wasted" for Odyle Energy, which refills all day; "Full, spend by Tue 16:00" for a daily refill) and "1 full" shows on the section and the title. After you spend some, correct it with the arrows (1 at a time, Odyle Energy 10) or click the figure and retype.
  • Back to empty. Empty the field, or click the × in it: the row returns to the dash and is ignored again.
  • Typing takes the keyboard from the game while the field is open, like the PvP name field; Enter, Esc or a click outside gives it back. Every other control is one click and never takes focus.
  • Groups. Sanctuary (three raids: attempts and final bosses) and Exploration (ten dungeons, 7 runs each) are one row with "2 of 6 done"; a click opens them. Their rows sit under the group's name. In edit mode each dungeon or raid can be hidden.
  • Daily Dungeon. The game calls Unknown Fissure a daily dungeon, but its 14 entries reset on Wednesday, so it is listed under This week as "Daily Dungeon · Unknown Fissure".
  • Characters. The list follows the character you play (the meter remembers name, server and class). The selector shows the others with what they have left; picking one shows its list. Duty Missions are marked Account: shared by your characters on one server.
  • Edit. The pencil turns the rows into show and hide buttons (hidden rows stay listed, faded) and adds a form below the list: a name, Tick or Count (with how many), Daily or Weekly, Add. Your own to-dos carry "Yours" and a delete button. The Membership box raises Odyle Energy's cap to 840. Duty Commands (20 a week, from Game8 only) start hidden.
  • Suggested +1 (option, off by default). When the meter sees a known dungeon's last boss fall, that count's row turns green and offers "+1?"; × lets it go. It never counts by itself. Needs a boss-to-to-do table in the bundled data, which does not exist yet.
  • Keyboard and screen readers (for the build, with GPUI's AccessKit roles): a tick row is a checkbox; a figure is a spin button with value, minimum and maximum, announced as "Daily Dungeon, Unknown Fissure, 9 of 14", Up and Down change it, Enter adds one (on an amount, Enter opens the field); the arrows are buttons named "One more: Daily Dungeon, Unknown Fissure"; sections and groups are buttons with an expanded state; an empty amount reads "Odyle Energy, not tracked". Tab goes through them in reading order. The ring: focus-ring, 2 px, 1 px outside.

Values proposed (not tokens yet)

  • Rows 26 px, 2 px apart, as in A; right inset 3 px on count rows so the arrows sit near the edge. Group rows: Lucide chevron-right in the box column; their rows indented 29 px (under the group's name).
  • Arrows: 20 × 20 px, 5 px radius (squarer than the 7 px tool button, as the bars and badges were made squarer), Lucide chevron-down / chevron-up 12 px, stroke 2.25, in muted; text while the pointer is on the row; pointer over: soft; pressed: cream at 0.20; at the limit: faint at 0.45.
  • Figure: 46 × 20 px, 5 px radius, 12.5 px / 600 with "/14" at 11 px / 500 faint; pointer over a count: soft; pointer over an amount: the input fill and edge (it opens a field). Empty amount: the dash in faint.
  • Field: 112 × 22 px, 6 px radius, input fill with the focus edge, the cap in faint, an 18 px × inside. Status text before the arrows: 11.5 px / 500 muted; full: warn 600 on the 0.12 warn row fill with the warning glyph.
  • Group summary "2 of 6 done": 11.5 px / 500 muted (good when all done). "+1?": the small text button on a good 0.16 fill.
