B: Focus you first, the party as context

  • The meter answers "how am I doing" before anything else: your DPS is the largest thing on screen, with your place and your pace against your best beside it.
  • One warm accent means you, everywhere: your row, your place, the capsule dot, a new best. Other players get neutral bars; their class shows only in the icon.
  • Soft, rounded and quiet in warm neutrals; the party list sits on its own darker inner plate so it holds over bright scenes.

Intended motion

  • Your hero number counts toward each new value over 250 ms and then rests; party figures swap without counting.
  • Rank change: rows slide over 280 ms (ease-out). Bars change width over 220 ms.
  • Pace changing between ahead and behind: colour cross-fades over 200 ms, no flash.
  • Capsule: on pointer over the pill grows into the card over 180 ms; back after a 350 ms grace period, as today. A new personal best appears once, without a pulse.

GPUI notes

  • All surfaces are solid or alpha fills with rounded corners, one 1 px border on the card and one drop shadow. The pill is rounded_full.
  • Fonts: Segoe UI Variable Display for the hero figure, Segoe UI Variable Text elsewhere, with tabular figures. GPUI selects optical-size families by name; tabular figures need the tnum font feature to be verified on Windows.
  • Count-up on the hero number is a per-frame redraw only while it runs (250 ms); no permanent frame loop.
  • Class icons are the existing PNGs, drawn as images with a 4 px corner radius. Other icons are single-colour Lucide SVGs.
