D2: Vanguard Soft bar-end and rank variants, switch them in the control bar

  • D, with the two parts that looked wrong replaced by switchable alternatives. Bar end, default d: soft rectangles whose fill brightens toward the leading edge, with no marker. Also available: a, the same fill on pill bars; b, an outlined flat fill; c, soft rectangles with C's straight edge line.
  • Rank, default b: small rounded-square badges. Also available: a, plain numerals; c, no rank column. Every option keeps a subtle cream rim around your bar. The capsule and the summary's skill bars follow the same choices.
  • Everything else is D unchanged: warm near-black card, compact figures, coral ten-segment gauge, sentence-case labels.

Intended motion

  • Bars grow and shrink over 240 ms (ease-out); the bright end (a), outline (b) or edge line (c) moves with the fill. Figures swap, no counting.
  • Rank change: rows slide over 240 ms. The boss gauge drains its last segment continuously; an emptied segment fades to the track over 200 ms.
  • The outcome chip and PB chip fade in over 150 ms; nothing flashes or pulses.
  • Capsule: pointer over grows the pill into the card over 180 ms; the rank keeps its place.

GPUI notes

  • Pills are rounded_full or a radius of half the height. Bar end a is a two-stop linear_gradient on the fill; b is a flat fill with border_1; c is a flat-ish two-stop fill plus a 2 px quad. The track clips the fill with overflow_hidden. The "you" rim is a 1 px border on the track.
  • Font: Bahnschrift at its SemiCondensed width, as in C, with the same caveat: selecting the variable font's named width instance through GPUI is unverified.
  • No uppercase transforms, no letter spacing, no text shadow, no blur. Class icons are the existing PNGs with a rounded clip; other icons are single-colour Lucide SVGs.
