C: Vanguard a bar chart you read from the corner of your eye

  • Each player is a solid bar in their class colour, so who is pulling ahead shows without reading a digit; the boss is a ten-segment gauge you can count at a glance.
  • Condensed Bahnschrift keeps names and short figures (61.2K) inside the bars; uppercase labels and square edges give it a HUD plate's discipline.
  • Every bar sits on its own dark track, and its leading edge is a full-strength tick, so position reads even when the fill is muted for text contrast.

Intended motion

  • Bars grow and shrink over 220 ms (ease-out); the leading-edge tick moves with them. Figures swap, no counting.
  • Rank change: rows trade places over 200 ms. The boss gauge drains its last segment continuously; a segment that empties dims over 150 ms.
  • Summary band appears with a 120 ms fade; no flashing on a kill or a new best.
  • Capsule: pointer over opens the plate downwards in one step; the rank block keeps its place so the eye does not lose it.

GPUI notes

  • The bar fill is a two-stop linear_gradient; the tick, track, rank block and gauge segments are plain quads. The "you" rim is a 1 px border on the track.
  • Font: Bahnschrift at its SemiCondensed width. GPUI asks DirectWrite for a family by name; whether it can select the SemiCondensed named instance of the variable font is unverified. Fallback: Bahnschrift at normal width, which costs about 12% of row width.
  • Uppercase labels are uppercased strings; no letter spacing is used. Square line caps on icons are the Lucide paths with a different stroke style, still one colour.
  • Class icons are the existing PNGs. Every other icon is a single-colour Lucide SVG.
