D: Vanguard Soft C's bar chart, rounded and calmed

  • Still C: every player is a bar in their class colour with a bright leading edge, figures are compact (61.2K), and the boss is a ten-segment gauge, so who leads reads from the corner of your eye.
  • Softer: pill bars and rank markers, a rounded warm near-black card, and sentence-case labels. The header and footer are plain rows, and status is shown as tinted chips, not full-width bands.
  • Calmer colour: borrowed from B's warm neutrals with a coral boss gauge. Class colour stays the only strong hue on screen.

Intended motion

  • Bars grow and shrink over 240 ms (ease-out) with the rounded cap riding the leading edge. Figures swap, no counting.
  • Rank change: rows slide over 240 ms. The boss gauge drains its last segment continuously; an emptied segment fades to the track over 200 ms.
  • The outcome chip and PB chip fade in over 150 ms; nothing flashes or pulses.
  • Capsule: pointer over grows the pill into the card over 180 ms; the rank marker keeps its place.

GPUI notes

  • Pills are rounded_full or a radius of half the height. The bar fill is a two-stop linear_gradient clipped by the track's rounding (overflow_hidden on a rounded div). The "you" rim is a 1 px border on the track.
  • Font: Bahnschrift at its SemiCondensed width, as in C, with the same caveat: selecting the variable font's named width instance through GPUI is unverified.
  • No uppercase transforms, no letter spacing, no text shadow, no blur. Class icons are the existing PNGs with a rounded clip; other icons are single-colour Lucide SVGs.
