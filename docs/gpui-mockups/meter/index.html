Meter overlay directions for the GPUI rebuild, round 1

  • Pick one direction; round 2 extends it to the PvP, detail and history overlays.
  • Each direction page shows every state (waiting, live with and without the pointer, capsule, both fight summaries) over a busy, a bright and a dark invented scene, with the app's opacity, scale and column options as live controls.
  • All data is invented. Only effects GPUI can draw are used in the overlays themselves; the scenes behind them stand in for the game.

A: Ledger

Dense table. Fixed, labelled columns and monospaced figures; class colour only as a thin rule under each row. For players who read numbers.

B: Focus

You first. Your DPS, place and pace are the hero; the party is a quiet list below. One warm accent means you, everything else is neutral.

C: Vanguard

Bar chart HUD. Solid class-coloured bars, condensed type, a ten-segment boss gauge. Reads from the corner of your eye.

D: Vanguard Soft

C, rounded and calmed. Pill bars and rank markers on a warm near-black card, sentence-case labels, tinted chips instead of bands. Class colour stays the only strong hue.

D2: Vanguard Soft, variants

D with switchable bar ends and rank styles. Shown here at its defaults: soft rectangles whose fill brightens toward the leading edge, and small rounded-square rank badges.
