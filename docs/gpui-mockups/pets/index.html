Level colours (round 2)

  • Proposed Values, all from the good token, no new hue. Badge: Lv 0 rgba(255, 240, 225, 0.09) with muted text (the rank badge's neutral); Lv 1 good at 0.10 with good text at 0.66; Lv 2 good at 0.28 with good text; Lv 3 good at 0.86 with ink text #17120d (a filled badge, as "you" is on the meter). Lv 0 to Lv 2 lay their tint over the track's dark plate (rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.34)), as names sit on the track. (b) also tints the fill: Lv 1 good 0.04 → 0.14, Lv 2 0.06 → 0.24, Lv 3 0.08 → 0.34; Lv 0 keeps the cream fill. In the card, the fact line's "Lv 2" becomes the same badge (16 px tall); (b) tints its 4 px bar too.
  • Recommended (a), the badge only. The badges form one column at the row's left edge, so the eye scans levels down that column; the fill keeps one meaning, progress toward the next level. In (b) every Lv 3 row is a full green bar, so a family with many maxed pets turns into a green wall where the pets still to farm (short, low bars) are the hardest rows to see, the opposite of what the list is for; and a green fill would also say "level" while its length says "progress".
  • The Lv 3 badge is filled, and Lv 0 to Lv 2 sit on the track's dark plate, so every badge stays readable over the bright scene at opacity 60 (section 2); without the plate, "Lv 1" and "Lv 2" washed out there in my first pass. Lv 1 and Lv 2 differ less from each other than from Lv 0 and Lv 3: the step that matters most in a long list is "maxed or not".

Behaviour (agreed in round 1)

  • The rule: 5 souls for Lv 0 → 1, 25 for 1 → 2, 75 for 2 → 3; Lv 3 is the cap on Global; souls beyond a level carry over (Lv 1 24/25 +5 → Lv 2 4/75). A pet at the cap reads "Lv 3 · 75/75". No insight-crystal figure anywhere.
  • The window lists every pet: tabs All, Cogni, Fera, Natura, Varian, Special, a search, pins on top, the game's order by default; "Closest to the next level" in the Show and Order menu.
  • The card appears when you gain souls of a pet and disappears 10 s after its last update. One card, pets in arrival order, at most three; any gain restarts the 10 s. The pointer over it pauses it; a click or × dismisses it; locked, it is click-through. It never takes focus.
  • Without the meter: the card sits 6 px under the toolbar while the meter is closed (above it when the toolbar is low), and 6 px above the meter while it is open; with a reminder card, the pet card stacks 6 px beyond it.
  • Keyboard and focus: the window never takes the keyboard from the game, except while the search field has it; Esc clears the search, a second Esc hands the keyboard back.
