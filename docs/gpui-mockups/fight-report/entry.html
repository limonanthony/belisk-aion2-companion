  • Decided: the approved fight summary card (310 px, after a boss fight) gets a full-width "Open the fight report" button under the skills, on the final card and on the provisional "Fight ended, may resume" card. "Report" beside Copy (2) and a tool button in the header (3) were not chosen; they stay at the end of this page as history.
  • Boss fights only: the summary card is shown for bosses, so every summary gets the button; other fights never do.
  • Decided: the approved fight history gets a report button on each boss record, and only there. "Fight report" only inside an opened record was not chosen.
  • The open pick: boss records get a small icon before the boss's name, so they stand out from mob records. Two candidates, side by side: a crown (recommended) and a skull; the "Boss icon" control above switches every History state. The crown, because the report already uses the skull for deaths (the Deaths column, the deaths list, a player's death plate): a skull on a record could read as "someone died in this fight".

Behaviour

  • Open the fight report opens the report window for that fight (one report window at a time, like the other windows: a second click brings it forward; a report for another fight replaces it). The summary card stays; × on the card still returns to the meter.
  • Why the wide button (decided): it is the one next step after a boss fight, so it gets a labelled, full-width target; the card grows by 32 px, only while the summary shows. Beside Copy it crowded the footer (in French Copy had to lose its label); in the header it was an unlabelled icon, easy to miss.
  • Locked (click-through): no report button, no Copy, no ×, as today. Unlock, or open the report from History.
  • Fight ended (provisional): the button is offered too; the report shows the fight as the meter has it, with the yellow chip.
  • History: the report button and the boss icon are on records of boss fights only (the record knows whether its target was a boss). Mob records keep both empty slots, so every name starts at the same edge and the dates line up. Records saved before this change still open a report, with Healing, Damage taken and Deaths marked "not recorded".
