A, tabs: Damage, Healing, Damage taken, Deaths not chosen: C, one table, was chosen on 2026-10-07; kept as exploration history

  • Not chosen. The user chose C, one table: "the table shows more infos without clicking". This page is kept as it was drawn, as the history of the choice.
  • The same 520 px window, header and key figures as B and C. Under them, four tabs (the approved segmented control): each is a ranked list of bars, like the meter's Target board, for one measure.
  • Damage: damage, DPS and share. Healing: healed, healing per second and share; players who cast no heal stay at the end with "—". Damage taken: what each player took, then the party's damage taken by monster skill. Deaths: who, when (mm:ss), killed by what, the last hit; the tab shows the count.
  • A click on a player in any tab opens their details inside the report.

Behaviour

  • The report opens on Damage; the tab is kept while the window lives. Each tab's list is sorted by its own measure; ranks and bars follow it.
  • Pointer on a row: the bar lifts by soft and a chevron appears at its end (enter and leave only). A click opens the player.
  • Keyboard (only through accessibility tools: the overlay windows never take focus from the game): Tab reaches the tabs (Left and Right switch), then the rows (Up and Down move, Enter opens). The ring: focus-ring, 2 px, 1 px outside.
  • A fight saved before this change: Healing, Damage taken and Deaths are dimmed and cannot be opened; a note says why.
  • Compared with C: one measure at a time, with more room for it (share column, the party's damage by source), but three of the four answers are a click away.
