C, one table, every figure approved 2026-10-07, kept as drawn for history; the approved version is approved/report.html

  • A new 520 px window, the same card, header, opacity, content scale and Barlow face as the detail and history windows. The header names the fight: "Fight report", the outcome, the boss, where, when and how long; then the party's key figures on one plate.
  • One row per player with everything on it: Damage, DPS, Healing, Damage taken (Taken) and Deaths. A click on a column header sorts by it; the bar and the rank follow the sorted column, so the table answers "who did the most damage", "who healed", "who took the hits" with one click each. A player with nothing in a column shows "—" and no bar; the name stays.
  • Deaths are listed under the table: when (mm:ss into the fight), who, which monster and skill killed them, and the last hit. Nobody died: one green line.
  • A click on a player (their row, or their line under Deaths) opens their details inside this window, with "‹ Report" to come back (decided): per-skill damage with crit counts, every hit type as count and rate, buff uptime as m:ss ("4:01"), their heals per skill and the damage they took by source.
  • Damage taken by source is per player only, in their details (decided); the report has no party-wide section for it.

Behaviour

  • Sorting (decided). Damage by default. A click on another header sorts by it, highest first (Deaths: most deaths first, then damage); the active header is text with a down arrow, the others are underlined at 0.25 (the approved sorting pattern). The sort is kept while the window is open. Damage and DPS are both shown because players who joined late have a high DPS on a low damage.
  • Rows are the meter's bars: rank badge (cream for you), class icon, name, the figures; the sorted column is text 13.5 / 600, the others muted 12.5. Bars use the player's class shade, scaled to the top value of the sorted column (floor 6%, none at zero).
  • Pointer on a row: the bar lifts by soft and a chevron appears at its end, on enter and leave only, never on movement inside it. A click opens that player. Death rows do the same.
  • Keyboard: like the other overlay windows, the report never takes focus from the game (non-activating), so the keyboard reaches it only through accessibility tools, as with the to-do window. Order: header sort buttons, rows, death rows; Up and Down move between rows; Enter opens the player. The ring: focus-ring, 2 px, 1 px outside the row. Every row is a button named "Saebrin, open details".
  • Many players: the table scrolls past 8 rows with the 3 px thumb; the deaths list stays under it.
  • Fights saved before this change (opened from History) have no healing, damage taken or deaths: those columns show "--", cannot be sorted, and a note says why. Damage, skills and buffs are complete.
  • Fight ended (the boss went quiet, may resume): the report shows what the meter has, with the yellow chip; if the fight resumes, the report window keeps the figures it opened with and the next summary offers a new report.

Values proposed (not tokens yet)

  • Window 520 px; columns Damage 54, DPS 46, Healing 52, Taken 50, Deaths 40 px, 6 px apart; the name keeps about 150 px (measured: a 26-character name and a server tag) before the ellipsis, about 105 px (17 characters) in a death row. Chevron slot 12 px at the bar's end, kept on every row so nothing shifts.
  • Death rows 24 px on track, 5 px radius: time 12.5 / 600 muted right-aligned in 32 px, player 128 px, "by" in faint, the monster in text and its skill in muted 11.5, the last hit 12 px muted. Deaths in a row: Lucide skull 11 px and the count in bad; zero in faint.
  • Lucide icons: file-text (title), skull, chevron-right, chevron-left (back), mouse-pointer (footer hint). file-text is already in crates/app/assets/icons/; the others would be added from Lucide.
