Pets window and soul tracker card approved 2026-10-10 (the pets round, TASK-34.3)

  • A window listing every pet with its level and its souls toward the next level, opened from the toolbar's paw-print; and a short-lived card that shows a pet's progress when you pick up its souls. Both work with the DPS meter closed: the game's pet messages are read always, like resources.
  • Chosen in the pets round (pets/README.md, the record of how it was chosen): window layout A (meter rows), card design A (the reminder card's twin), the name "Pets" (French draft "Familiers"), level colours on the badge only (round 2, variant (a)). Settings › Pets is in settings.html.
  • Values are in tokens.md, "Pets". Pet names, families, levels and souls are invented except seven public names (the pet catalogue, TASK-34.2, supplies the real ones); the clock is Monday 21:48, logged in at 19:02 on Ossia.

The window

  • The rule: 5 souls for Lv 0 → 1, 25 for 1 → 2, 75 for 2 → 3; Lv 3 is the cap on Global; souls beyond a level carry over (Lv 1 24/25 +5 → Lv 2 4/75). A pet at the cap reads "75/75 Max". No insight-crystal figure anywhere.
  • Frame: the to-do window's 370 px with Lucide paw-print and "Pets"; the source line ("From the game, live · logged in at 19:02 · Ossia"; "As of your login Sun 22:40 · relog to refresh" when the collection was read at an earlier login, with a tooltip; "No pets read yet. Log in while Belisk runs." before any read); the search and its filter button; the tabs All, Cogni, Fera, Natura, Varian, Special; the family summary (owned of the tab's pets, how many at Lv 3, and the levels gained as a 4 px bar); the list, scrolling past 452 px; the footer "Souls per level 5 · 25 · 75 · Lv 3 is the cap".
  • Rows: the meter's row. The level badge "Lv 2" is coloured by level: Lv 0 grey, Lv 1 and Lv 2 greens, Lv 3 a filled green with ink text; "Lv N" always stays, so colour is never the only cue. The track's fill is the souls toward the next level in the neutral cream, so it means only progress; then the name, "27/75" and "→ Lv 3" ("Max" at the cap). A pet never obtained reads "Lv 0 · 0/5", its name in muted. Special pets give no stats: a faint "no stats" after the name.
  • Order: the game's order (pet ids), so the list compares with the game's Pets window row by row. The Show and Order menu (the filter button): Show all pets / not owned yet / not at Lv 3; Order: game order / closest to the next level (fewest souls left first). A filter in force lights the button with the dot and adds a line saying what is kept and how many, with Clear.
  • Pins: the pin button shows while the pointer is in the row (enter and leave, never on moves inside) and stays shown on a pinned pet; tooltip "Pin to the top" / "Unpin". Pinned pets sit on top of All and of their own family's tab, under "Pinned", in the order pinned; kept per server, with the collection.
  • Search: matches anywhere in the name, in the current tab, pinned first; no match in a family offers "Search all families".
  • Per server: the collection is shared by your characters on one server; the window shows the server of the character in game. The game sends the whole collection only at login; between logins, the gains Belisk sees are added.
  • Keyboard and focus: the window never takes the keyboard from the game, except while the search field has it (a click in it, the PvP name field's rule); Esc clears the search, a second Esc hands the keyboard back. For accessibility tools: Tab reaches the search, the filter button, the tabs (Left and Right) and the pins (Up and Down, Space pins), the focus ring 1 px outside the control.
  • Window: an overlay window like the to-do window (no-activate, tool window); opened and closed from the toolbar's paw-print (lit while open), under the toolbar the first time, then where you left it.

The soul tracker card

  • When: on a soul pickup (and a black-market purchase, +5, the same game message), with the meter open or closed. Settings › Pets can turn it off, limit it to pinned pets, and leave out pets at Lv 3 or black-market buys.
  • Look: the reminder card's frame and header (Lucide paw-print, "Pet souls", the timer ring, × when unlocked); per pet, starting at the paw icon's left edge, the name, then the level badge with "28/75 → Lv 3", then a 4 px bar, with 8 px of visible space between the name's letters and the badge and between the badge and the bar (approved 2026-10-10, pet-card-layout/); a level reached adds a good chip "Lv 2 reached", a first unlock "Unlocked"; at the cap "Lv 3 · 75/75", the bar full.
  • Lifetime: it disappears 10 s after its last update. The timer is the 12 px hollow ring left of the × (approved 2026-10-10, card-timer/; it replaced the 2 px time-left line, which read as a second bar under the pets' progress bars): it empties clockwise from 12 o'clock and is full again at each gain. The pointer over the card pauses it (the arc turns text) and lights the ×; a click or × dismisses it. Locked, it is click-through with no ×, and the ring takes the ×'s place. It never takes focus.
  • Several pets: one card, pets in arrival order (a newcomer is added at the end, nothing moves), at most three (a fourth replaces the oldest).
  • Place: the reminder card's: 6 px above the meter while it is open (below it when there is no room), 6 px under the toolbar while it is closed (above it when the toolbar sits low), 310 px. With a reminder card on screen, the reminder keeps its place and the pet card stacks 6 px beyond it.
  • Motion: fades in and out over 150 ms; the bar grows over 240 ms; figures swap in place; nothing flashes or pulses.
