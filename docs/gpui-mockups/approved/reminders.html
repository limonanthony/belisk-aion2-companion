Reminder card approved 2026-10-05

  • Its own small overlay window just above the meter (just below it when the meter sits at the top of the screen), on the meter's left edge and at its width; same translucent card, opacity and content scale as the meter. It never takes focus.
  • A quiet header says when ("In 1 min · 16:00"); under it, each event's name in bold and one line of fact from the game data. Events at the same minute share one card and one sound, the rarer event first.
  • It goes by itself after 30 s; a thin line at the bottom drains over those 30 s. Unlocked, a click anywhere on the card, or its ×, dismisses it; locked, it is click-through and simply runs out. All names and figures are invented except the event facts (from the game client's tables).

Behaviour

  • When. At the lead time chosen in Settings (1 min by default) before each event switched on. The header reads "In 1 min" rounded up to the minute, so it does not tick while the card is up; the clock time beside it never changes.
  • Same minute. All events due at that minute go into one card with one sound. Order: weekly, then daily, then every 3 hours, then hourly (the rarer event matters more). With everything on, three can meet: 11:00 (Arena of Tactics, Rift portals, Shugo Festival), 16:00 (reward keys, Watcher Kaira, Shugo Festival), 19:00 (Arena of Tactics, Watcher Kaira, Shugo Festival). Three on one card is fine.
  • Placement. 6 px above the meter's top edge, left edges aligned. If the card does not fit above the meter on its monitor, it goes 6 px below the meter's bottom edge and follows the meter's height. In the capsule layout, a fixed 220 px card on the capsule's left edge.
  • Dismissal. Unlocked: a click anywhere on the card or on × closes it at once; the pointer over the card pauses the 30 s (the line stops) and lights the ×. Locked: the card is click-through like the meter, shows no × and simply runs out. A newer reminder replaces an older one still on screen.
  • Focus. The window never activates (same no-activate, tool-window styles as the meter); the game keeps keyboard and mouse focus. No Windows notification is produced.

Values

  • In tokens.md, "Reminder card": widths 310, 340 and 220 px, heights 84, 123 and 162 px, the header, the event block, the time-left line and the 150 ms fades.

Not built yet

  • Meter hidden between fights: where the card goes while the meter is hidden is decided when that feature is built.
  • Alt+E (show or hide the overlay) dismissing a reminder: comes with that shortcut.

GPUI notes

  • A second overlay window with the meter's styles (transparent, no-activate, tool window, click-through while locked), sized to the card like the meter (fit-to-card); positioned from the meter window's bounds in device pixels.
  • The fade is the card's opacity tween inside the window; the window itself appears at full size, transparent. The line is a 2 px div whose width is recomputed per frame only while the card is up.
