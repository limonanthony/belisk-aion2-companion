Reminder card approved 2026-10-05; placement while the meter is closed approved 2026-10-08; the timer ring, equal padding and the body at the bell's edge 2026-10-10

  • Changed 2026-10-08 (hub round, hub/): the meter is now a tool the user opens from the toolbar. While the meter is closed the card sits 6 px under the toolbar, left edges aligned (above it when the toolbar is low on the screen), at 310 px (toolbar.html, "A reminder while the meter is closed"); while the meter is open, as drawn here. The toolbar's bell lists the next reminders and pauses them.
  • Its own small overlay window just above the meter (just below it when the meter sits at the top of the screen), on the meter's left edge and at its width; same translucent card, opacity and content scale as the meter. It never takes focus.
  • A quiet header says when ("In 1 min · 16:00"); under it, starting at the bell's left edge (2026-10-10, as the soul tracker card: pet-card-layout/), each event's name in bold and right under it one line of fact from the game data. Events at the same minute share one card and one sound, the rarer event first.
  • It goes by itself after 30 s; a small hollow ring left of the × empties over those 30 s (approved 2026-10-10, card-timer/; it replaced the thin line at the bottom, so the reminder card and the soul tracker card share one timer). Unlocked, a click anywhere on the card, or its ×, dismisses it; locked, it is click-through and simply runs out. All names and figures are invented except the event facts (from the game client's tables).

Behaviour

  • When. At the lead time chosen in Settings (1 min by default) before each event switched on. The header reads "In 1 min" rounded up to the minute, so it does not tick while the card is up; the clock time beside it never changes.
  • Same minute. All events due at that minute go into one card with one sound. Order: weekly, then daily, then every 3 hours, then hourly (the rarer event matters more). With everything on, three can meet: 11:00 (Arena of Tactics, Rift portals, Shugo Festival), 16:00 (reward keys, Watcher Kaira, Shugo Festival), 19:00 (Arena of Tactics, Watcher Kaira, Shugo Festival). Three on one card is fine.
  • Placement. 6 px above the meter's top edge, left edges aligned. If the card does not fit above the meter on its monitor, it goes 6 px below the meter's bottom edge and follows the meter's height. In the capsule layout, a fixed 220 px card on the capsule's left edge.
  • Dismissal. Unlocked: a click anywhere on the card or on × closes it at once; the pointer over the card pauses the 30 s (the ring stops and its arc turns text) and lights the ×. Locked: the card is click-through like the meter, shows no × (the ring takes its place) and simply runs out. A newer reminder replaces an older one still on screen.
  • Focus. The window never activates (same no-activate, tool-window styles as the meter); the game keeps keyboard and mouse focus. No Windows notification is produced.

Values

  • In tokens.md, "Reminder card": widths 310, 340 and 220 px, heights 81, 120 and 159 px, padding 9 / 8 / 12 px (14 px from the outer edge to the content on every side), the header, the event block, the timer ring and the 150 ms fades.
