Meter overlay approved, rounds 1, 2 and 4

  • Each player is a soft-cornered bar in class colour that brightens toward its leading edge; rank is a small rounded-square badge, cream for you, with a cream rim on your bar. Figures are compact (61.2K); the target's health is a ten-segment gauge.
  • Two boards, chosen by themselves: Session in the open world (everyone's damage since the last reset, rows stable across mobs) and Target on a boss. The footer's Session | Target switch shows which; clicking the other side pins it, and a dot marks the pin.
  • The header names what is being hit, at full width. Under the pointer the tools (reset, capsule, lock, copy, …, close, evenly spaced) take the time's place at top right; the window never resizes. Locked: no tools, and the switch does not react. Values are in tokens.md; all names and figures are invented.

Behaviour

  • Automatic board. Target while a boss is engaged (bundled list, the HP rule or your corrections); Session otherwise. The first hit on a boss engages it, and the Target board then stays on that boss whatever else is hit, until it dies, another boss is engaged, it goes two minutes without a hit, or you reset. (A zone change would release it as well, but the game does not re-send your identity on a zone, so in practice the two-minute rule lets go after you leave.) Clicking the inactive side of the switch pins it; clicking the active side again returns to automatic. A reset or the next boss also clears the pin. A pinned Target board follows your current target.
  • Session board. Everyone's damage across all targets since the last reset or idle reset. Bold figure: damage, sorted by damage; quiet figure: DPS with a faint "/s", over combat time (each player's fights summed). Percent: share of session damage (there is no single target HP for contribution). The header time is the session's combat time. Footer: session damage and the number of targets fought.
  • Target board footer. With a personal best for this boss, the left side shows the pace against it once the fight is 10 s old ("−6.8% vs PB", green ahead, rose behind). Without one, or before 10 s, it shows the party's damage on this target.
  • Not identified yet. The game sends your character's name at login, so the meter has to be running before you log in (returning to character select and back may also work). Until then rows show ID:<n>, nobody is marked as you, the capsule shows the party's figure without a rank badge, and a quiet line above the footer says "Start before login, or relog via character select".
  • "…" menu. The boss correction for the target in the header (Treat as a boss / Not a boss / Automatic); History, PvP and Settings will join it later. A click elsewhere in the meter, a second click on "…", or the pointer leaving the card closes it.

Intended motion

  • Bars grow and shrink over 240 ms (ease-out). Figures swap, no counting. Rank change: rows slide over 240 ms. The gauge drains its last segment continuously; an emptied segment fades to the track over 200 ms.
  • Pointer over: the time cross-fades to the tools in 100 ms and back when the pointer leaves; the window keeps its size.
  • Board change (automatic, pin, unpin): the board cross-fades in 150 ms and the switch's highlight moves across in 120 ms. The card's height follows the board (the Target board adds the gauge line), like any row-count change; it never changes on hover.
  • The tooltip over the switch appears after GPUI's usual tooltip delay and fades in over 150 ms. The outcome chip and PB chip fade in over 150 ms; nothing flashes or pulses. Capsule: pointer over grows the pill into the card over 180 ms.

GPUI notes

  • The bar fill is a two-stop linear_gradient clipped by the track (overflow_hidden on a 5 px rounded div). The "you" rim is a 1 px border on the track. Pills are rounded_full.
  • The switch is two divs in a rounded track, each with a hover fill and an on_click; the pin dot is a 4 px rounded_full div. Locked, the window is click-through, so nothing hovers.
  • The tooltip is drawn inside the overlay window above the footer and needs about two rows of room; with one row it sits beside the switch.
  • Font: Barlow Semi Condensed, embedded (this page loads the same files). No uppercase transforms, no letter spacing, no text shadow, no blur, no card shadow. Class icons are the PNGs with a rounded clip; other icons are single-colour Lucide SVGs.
