Meter overlay approved, rounds 1 and 2

  • Each player is a soft-cornered bar in class colour that brightens toward its leading edge, so who leads reads from the corner of your eye. Figures are compact (61.2K); the boss is a ten-segment gauge.
  • Rank is a small rounded-square badge, cream for you, and a subtle cream rim marks your bar. Under the pointer the seven controls take the gauge's line, so the boss name keeps its full width and the window never resizes. Combat power beside names at 0.82.
  • The controls above are the app's real overlay settings. Values are recorded in tokens.md.

Intended motion

  • Bars grow and shrink over 240 ms (ease-out). Figures swap, no counting.
  • Rank change: rows slide over 240 ms. The boss gauge drains its last segment continuously; an emptied segment fades to the track over 200 ms.
  • Pointer over: the gauge line cross-fades to the tool row in 100 ms and back when the pointer leaves; the window keeps its height.
  • The outcome chip and PB chip fade in over 150 ms; nothing flashes or pulses. Capsule: pointer over grows the pill into the card over 180 ms.

GPUI notes

  • The bar fill is a two-stop linear_gradient clipped by the track (overflow_hidden on a 5 px rounded div). The "you" rim is a 1 px border on the track. Pills are rounded_full.
  • Font: Bahnschrift at its SemiCondensed width. Selecting the variable font's named width instance through GPUI is unverified; tabular figures need the tnum feature.
  • No uppercase transforms, no letter spacing, no text shadow, no blur. Class icons are the existing PNGs with a rounded clip; other icons are single-colour Lucide SVGs.
