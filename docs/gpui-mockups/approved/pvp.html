PvP overlay approved, round 2

  • The players you watch are pinned on top with their health, your last attacker first; kill stats and damage dealt and taken share two tabs below. In a fight, the watched players' health is what you glance at.
  • Kill stats sort by damage or kills (with assists and deaths); Clear empties them at once. Clicking a dealt or taken row opens the player detail; the eye button adds that player to the watch list.
  • Same language as the meter: class-coloured soft bars where the class is known, a quieter cream bar where it is not (kill stats), coral for health. Values are in tokens.md.

Behaviour carried over

  • Kill stats sort by damage or kills from their column headers; ties fall back to kills, assists, fewer deaths, then name. Clear empties them at once, with no confirmation: it only clears the running PvP tally.
  • Capture is passive: players out of range are not counted, and damage settles when the target dies. The info button shows that line; the empty state shows it directly.
  • The watch list polls every 500 ms. Your last attacker is added on top and cannot be removed; a watched name not in range shows as "Not in range". The add field shows only while adding (the + button in the Watching row) or while the list is empty; suggestions come from players seen this session.

GPUI notes

  • The suggestion list is a popover inside the overlay window, like any menu here; the window must leave room for it. The text field is a GPUI input with its own caret.
  • Tabs are a segmented control drawn with two fills; no new primitive.
