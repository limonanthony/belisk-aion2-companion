Approved screens reference for the GPUI implementation

  • Only designs the user has validated live here. Explorations stay in meter/ (round 1), round2/, round3/ and round4/.
  • The product is called "AION 2 DPS Meter" (title bar, About); overlays say "DPS Meter" where space is tight. Aether and NOIA2 appear only as the projects it is forked from, in About.
  • Values are in tokens.md; a screen and its tokens change together.

Startup gate

Validated in round 3. Administrator rights, Npcap and WinDivert with the weighting from preflight.rs; Install Npcap with the SHA-256 check; waits for "Open the meter" when Npcap is missing so the recommendation is read. States: checking, all good, Npcap missing, nothing can capture, installing, install refused, not elevated, re-run from Home.

Home

Validated in round 3. Sidebar navigation; one card with the status, character and server on one line, elapsed time, Start or Stop and "Close this window once it starts"; the in-game shortcuts beside it. States: ready, starting, waiting for the game, running, start failed. English and French.

Settings

Validated in round 3. Overlay (with the live preview), Meter behaviour, Shortcuts (recorder, conflict), Capture (status, backend, recording, replay, log folder), Personal bests (reset confirm, empty), Reminders (added 2026-10-05: master switch, lead time, sound, test, one switch per event; rows dim when reminders are off), To-dos (added 2026-10-06: daily reset hour, membership, restore the default to-dos, reset them completely with a confirm dialog), General (language), About (licence, credits, storage, clear-cache dialog).

Meter overlay

Validated in rounds 1 and 2 (direction D2, bar end d, rank b, combat power at 0.82) and round 4 (layout D). Two boards chosen by themselves: Session in the open world, Target on a boss, with a Session | Target switch in the always-on footer to pin either. The target's name on top; under the pointer the tools take the time's place at top right.

States: Session at rest, pointer over, switch hover, Copy; Target with and without a personal best, pointer over, locked; pinned both ways; not identified yet; long boss name; waiting; capsule on Session, on a boss and waiting; the fight summaries (defeated, pointer on the report button, fight ended, party, French, locked). Live controls: background opacity, content scale, rows, mask names, server, total damage, combat power, contribution or share.

Added 2026-10-07 with the fight report: "Open the fight report", a full-width button under the skills on every summary card (also "Fight ended, may resume"); none while locked.

PvP overlay

Validated in round 2. The watch list is pinned on top (your last attacker first); Kill stats and Damage share two tabs below. States: just opened, kill stats by damage and by kills, damage dealt and taken, adding to the watch list, long list.

Player detail

Validated in round 2. Skill table with Hits, Crit and Total; a row opens to the other twelve figures. States: nothing selected, live, skill opened, buffs open, history mode, no skill data.

Fight history

Validated in round 2. Two lines per record; deleting one or all asks first on the button itself, with Cancel. Added 2026-10-07 with the fight report: boss records have a crown before the boss's name and a report button before Delete; mob records keep both slots empty. States: empty, records, filtering, record opened, deleting one, deleting all, pointer on a report button, keyboard focus on one, after deleting all.

Fight report

Validated 2026-10-07. Boss fights only: opened from "Open the fight report" on the summary card or a boss record's report button in History. Its own 520 px overlay window: "Fight report", the outcome, the boss, where, when, players and time, the party's key figures, then one row per player with Damage, DPS, Healing, Taken and Deaths. A click on a header sorts by it (Damage first, kept while the window is open); the deaths are listed under the table.

A click on a player (or their death) opens their details inside the window, with "‹ Report" back: damage per skill with crits, every hit type as count and rate, buff uptime as m:ss, heals per skill, damage taken by monster skill, their deaths. Plates keep figures left, tables right. Fights saved before the report existed say "not recorded" for Healing, Taken and Deaths. States: the report sorted three ways, pointer and keyboard on a row, no deaths and several, 14 players, long names, fight ended, not recorded; then every player-details tab and state.

Reminder card

Validated 2026-10-05. Its own overlay window 6 px above the meter (below it when the meter is at the top of the screen), on the meter's left edge and at its width; a fixed 220 px card in the capsule layout. A "In 1 min · 16:00" header, then each event's name and one line of fact; events at the same minute share one card, the rarer first.

Goes by itself after 30 s, with a thin line draining over them; the pointer over it pauses. Unlocked, a click or its × dismisses it; locked, it is click-through with no ×. States: one, two and three events, 5 min ahead, pointer over, last seconds, capsule, below the meter, locked, test, live.

To-do window

Validated 2026-10-06 (direction E). Its own 370 px overlay window, opened from "To-dos" in the meter's "…" menu: one list per character, in folding sections Today, Recharges and This week, each with its countdown. Everything is set by hand: ticks, counts with one-less and one-more arrows (a click on the figure adds one), and optional amounts that stay empty until typed, then refill on schedule and turn yellow when full.

A done to-do is struck through and muted where it stands. A click anywhere on a tick row marks it done, with a check ("Mark done", "Undo") at its end under the pointer; counts are done at N. A × under the pointer hides a row, "N hidden · Show" brings rows back, "+ Add a to-do" ends the list. States: fresh week, under way, pointer on a to-do and a done one, a count at its limit, pressing, an amount entered, typing, full, hidden, after Show, adding, removing your own, everything done, keyboard focus, character selector, opening it, try it.

Design tokens in short

  • Card: warm near-black rgba(16, 14, 12, a), where a is the user's opacity (default 150/255); 14 px radius; 1 px border rgba(255, 236, 214, 0.10); no drop shadow (the window ends at the card).
  • Bars: 22 px rows with a 3 px gap, 5 px radius on track and fill, track rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.34), fill a two-stop gradient of the class colour from 0.14 to 0.50, brightest at the leading edge. No edge marker.
  • Rank: 18 px badge with 4 px radius, rgba(255, 240, 225, 0.09); yours is cream rgba(255, 246, 236, 0.90) with dark text, and your bar gets a 1 px cream rim at 0.45. 8 px on each side of the badge.
  • Type: overlays in Barlow Semi Condensed (embedded; the meter page loads it, the other overlay pages still draw with Bahnschrift); the main window in Segoe UI Variable for text with Bahnschrift for titles, figures and buttons. Tabular figures, sentence case.
  • Meter footer: always shown; figures on the left (Session: damage and targets fought; Target: pace against your best, or party damage), ping and a 100 px Session | Target switch on the right, with a 4 px dot when pinned by hand.
  • Text: rgba(255, 246, 236, …) at 0.96, 0.82 (combat power), 0.66 and 0.44. Accents: good #86e3a8, warn #f2c66d, bad #f7a8a8, boss HP #e8735f, new PB #fde68a on amber 0.17. Class colours appear only on bars, coverage bars and the capsule badge.
  • Overlay window parts: segmented tabs, 26 px inputs and selects, popovers on rgba(30, 26, 22, 0.98), a "Delete?" danger fill with Cancel for confirmations, rows that open to a four-column figure block.
  • Main window: opaque canvas #141210, sidebar and title bar #0e0c0b, groups #1b1815 with 10 px radius; one cream primary button (#f3ebe1 on #17120d) per view; cream switches and sliders.
  • Reminder card: the meter's card at the meter's width (220 px over the capsule), 6 px above it; bell header, 13 px event names, 11.5 px facts, a 2 px time-left line; 150 ms fades, 30 s, paused under the pointer.
  • To-do window: the overlay card at 370 px; 26 px rows 2 px apart with a 12 px gutter so names line up under the section labels; done is a 1 px line through the name in muted; a 20 px check and × at the row's end under the pointer only; counts as a 46 px figure between two 20 px arrows; a full amount's row on rgba(242, 198, 109, 0.12).
  • Fight report: the overlay card at 520 px (the detail window's); one player table with 54/46/52/50/40 px figure columns 6 px apart, the sorted one text 13.5 / 600, the others muted 12.5; 24 px death rows on track; figures left on plates and right in tables; "--" in faint for what was not recorded. On the summary card a 24 px full-width soft button; in History a 12 px Lucide crown in hp and a 20 px report button on boss records.
