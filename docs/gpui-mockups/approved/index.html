Approved screens reference for the GPUI implementation

  • Only designs the user has validated live here, one current design per screen. Explorations stay in meter/ (round 1), round2/, round3/, round4/, fight-report/, hub/, todos-autofill/ and updates/.
  • The product is Belisk, after the Kibelisk players plant to respawn (decided 2026-10-09; it was "AION 2 DPS Meter"; the "Kisk" idea was dropped). Its tagline: "Belisk — combat meter and companion for AION 2" (French "Belisk — compteur de combat et compagnon pour AION 2"), the description in Settings › About. The name is the Fluent term -app-name, and every approved page takes it from one constant, app-name.js: the main window's title bar and the startup gate, About, the toolbar ("Quit Belisk", its accessible name, "Log in while Belisk runs."), the first-open prompt, the tray, the source lines of the Resources and to-do windows, the Settings descriptions and the page notes. Where the meter has no target to name (waiting card, idle capsule) it says "DPS meter" (French "Compteur DPS"), the toolbar's name for the tool. Aether and NOIA2 appear only as the projects it is forked from, in About.
  • Values are in tokens.md; a screen and its tokens change together.

Startup gate

Validated in round 3. Administrator rights, Npcap and WinDivert with the weighting from preflight.rs; Install Npcap with the SHA-256 check; waits for "Open the meter" when Npcap is missing so the recommendation is read. States: checking, all good, Npcap missing, nothing can capture, installing, install refused, not elevated, re-run from Home.

Home

Validated in round 3. Sidebar navigation; one card with the status, character and server on one line, elapsed time, Start or Stop and "Close this window once it starts"; the in-game shortcuts beside it. States: ready, starting, waiting for the game, running, start failed. English and French.

Toolbar

Validated 2026-10-08 (the hub). The app opens with this horizontal floating bar alone; every tool opens and closes from it: DPS meter (crossed swords), PvP meter (crosshair), To-dos, Fight history (crown), Reminders (bell, with a dropdown of the next reminders, Pause and Reminder settings), Settings. Odyle Energy and Shugo keys always shown ("245/840 +380"); a warning with a number and a dropdown while other resources are full and their refill is near (1 h for Odyle, 3 h for daily refills, 24 h for the Daily Dungeon's weekly one; further away a full count looks normal). Fold to a handle; × quits the app (its tooltip names it with -app-name). Never takes the keyboard; Alt+Shift+CapsLock hides and shows everything. States: at launch, pointer on a tool, the meter stepped aside, tools open, count tooltips, full near the refill and not near, the warning and its dropdown, Resources open, no value yet, the bell's dropdown, paused, a reminder under it, quit, folded, low on the screen, French, keyboard focus, try it.

Added 2026-10-09: with Settings › Overlay › "Hide between fights" on, an open meter steps aside between fights; its tool stays lit and its tooltip says "Hidden between fights. Click to show it for 10 s." (state "Pointer on the meter, stepped aside"). The same day, the update icon (the updates round): first in the end group before fold, only while an update is available, downloading (a 2 px progress line; paused while you fight) or ready to install (Lucide rotate-cw in good); a click downloads, opens Settings › About, or restarts into the new version. States: available, downloading, paused, checking the download, ready, ready during a fight, failed its check, beside the warning, folded, keyboard focus, French, after the restart, try the update.

Meter overlay

Validated in rounds 1 and 2 (direction D2, bar end d, rank b, combat power at 0.82) and round 4 (layout D). Two boards chosen by themselves: Session in the open world, Target on a boss, with a Session | Target switch in the always-on footer to pin either. The target's name on top; under the pointer the tools take the time's place at top right.

States: Session at rest, pointer over, switch hover, Copy; Target with and without a personal best, pointer over, locked; pinned both ways; not identified yet; long boss name; waiting; capsule on Session, on a boss and waiting; the fight summaries (defeated, pointer on the report button, fight ended, party, French, locked). Live controls: background opacity, content scale, rows, mask names, server, total damage, combat power, contribution or share.

Added 2026-10-07 with the fight report: "Open the fight report", a full-width button under the skills on every summary card (also "Fight ended, may resume"); none while locked.

Changed 2026-10-08 with the hub: opened from the toolbar; "…" holds only the boss setting ("Treat … as a boss", "Not a boss", "Automatic"); × closes the meter only, and fights are tracked only while it is open.

First-open prompt

Validated 2026-10-08 (the hub). The first time the meter is opened from the toolbar, a card 6 px above it (placed as the reminder card) asks "Open the meter at launch?" and names Settings › Overlay › Open the meter at launch; "Not now" and "Yes, at launch". Either answer is final; Yes shows a good note for 4 s. States: first open, pointer on Yes, after Yes, French, capsule layout.

What's new

Validated 2026-10-09 (the updates round). After an update, an overlay window with History's frame opens once by itself, 6 px under the toolbar (above it when the toolbar is low): the versions newer than the last one you ran, newest first, all open (version and date, a summary, bullets), from CHANGELOG.md / CHANGELOG.fr.md, which start at 1.0.0, the version reset. "Full changelog" opens Settings › About › Changelog. Never during a fight; nothing on a fresh install; a settings file from before the reset sees 1.0.0's notes once. States: one version, skipped versions, scrolled, pointer on close and Full changelog, toolbar low, meter open, during a fight, fresh install, from before the reset, French, the Markdown subset beside its source.

Fight report

Validated 2026-10-07. Boss fights only: opened from "Open the fight report" on the summary card or a boss record's report button in History. Its own 520 px overlay window: "Fight report", the outcome, the boss, where, when, players and time, the party's key figures, then one row per player with Damage, DPS, Healing, Taken and Deaths. A click on a header sorts by it (Damage first, kept while the window is open); the deaths are listed under the table.

A click on a player (or their death) opens their details inside the window, with "‹ Report" back: damage per skill with crits, every hit type as count and rate, buff uptime as m:ss, heals per skill, damage taken by monster skill, their deaths. Plates keep figures left, tables right. Fights saved before the report existed say "not recorded" for Healing, Taken and Deaths. States: the report sorted three ways, pointer and keyboard on a row, no deaths and several, 14 players, long names, fight ended, not recorded; then every player-details tab and state.

Reminder card

Validated 2026-10-05. Its own overlay window 6 px above the meter (below it when the meter is at the top of the screen), on the meter's left edge and at its width; a fixed 220 px card in the capsule layout. A "In 1 min · 16:00" header, then each event's name and one line of fact; events at the same minute share one card, the rarer first.

Goes by itself after 30 s, with a thin line draining over them; the pointer over it pauses. Unlocked, a click or its × dismisses it; locked, it is click-through with no ×. States: one, two and three events, 5 min ahead, pointer over, last seconds, capsule, below the meter, locked, test, live. Since the hub (2026-10-08), while the meter is closed it sits 6 px under the toolbar (toolbar.html).

To-do window

Validated 2026-10-06 (direction E); values from the game and opening from the toolbar validated 2026-10-08. Its own 370 px overlay window, opened from the toolbar's checklist: one list per character, in folding sections Today and This week, each with its countdown. Changed 2026-10-08 ("I think we can remove the recharges from the todos now no ?"): no Recharges section; Odyle Energy, Shugo keys, Invasion keys, Nightmare and the Daily Dungeon are in the Resources window only (and the toolbar), and the title and the character list count to-dos only. When you log in while Belisk runs, the game fills the weekly entries (Ascension Trial, done of target, "+1" extra beside it); game values have no arrows, a click types over them, and a line under the character selector says where they come from. Duty Missions, Duty Commands and your own to-dos stay yours to count, with one-less and one-more arrows. Duty Missions, shared by your characters on a server, carry the Account icon alone, the word under the pointer.

A done to-do is struck through and muted where it stands. A click anywhere on a tick row marks it done, with a check ("Mark done", "Undo") at its end under the pointer; counts are done at N. A × under the pointer hides a row, "N hidden · Show" brings rows back, "+ Add a to-do" ends the list. Settled 2026-10-08: This week keeps only Ascension Trial, Duty Commands (hidden at first) and your own to-dos; Transcendence, Conquest final boss kills, Cradle of Nihility, Dying Dramata's Nest, Ordeal, Subjugation, Sanctuary and Exploration are gone (no cap in game, or not in the game's menu), and with them the folding groups. States: filled from the game, pointer on the Account icon, pointer on a game value, this week done, a game value next to a manual one, pointer on a to-do and a done one, a count at its limit, pressing, from yesterday's login, typing over a game value, after it, just logged in, before the first login, hidden, after Show, adding, removing your own, everything done, fresh week, keyboard focus on a row and on a game figure, character selector, opening it, try it.

Tray icon

Validated 2026-10-08 (the hub). The app's icon in the notification area: tooltip -app-name ("Belisk"); a left click or "Show toolbar" (the default item) brings back the toolbar and every open tool; "Quit" ends the process. Windows draws the tooltip and the menu.

Design tokens in short

  • Card: warm near-black rgba(16, 14, 12, a), where a is the user's opacity (default 150/255); 14 px radius; 1 px border rgba(255, 236, 214, 0.10); no drop shadow (the window ends at the card).
  • Bars: 22 px rows with a 3 px gap, 5 px radius on track and fill, track rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.34), fill a two-stop gradient of the class colour from 0.14 to 0.50, brightest at the leading edge. No edge marker.
  • Rank: 18 px badge with 4 px radius, rgba(255, 240, 225, 0.09); yours is cream rgba(255, 246, 236, 0.90) with dark text, and your bar gets a 1 px cream rim at 0.45. 8 px on each side of the badge.
  • Type: overlays in Barlow Semi Condensed (embedded; the meter page loads it, the other overlay pages still draw with Bahnschrift); the main window in Segoe UI Variable for text with Bahnschrift for titles, figures and buttons. Tabular figures, sentence case.
  • Meter footer: always shown; figures on the left (Session: damage and targets fought; Target: pace against your best, or party damage), ping and a 100 px Session | Target switch on the right, with a 4 px dot when pinned by hand.
  • Text: rgba(255, 246, 236, …) at 0.96, 0.82 (combat power), 0.66 and 0.44. Accents: good #86e3a8, warn #f2c66d, bad #f7a8a8, boss HP #e8735f, new PB #fde68a on amber 0.17. Class colours appear only on bars, coverage bars and the capsule badge.
  • Overlay window parts: segmented tabs, 26 px inputs and selects, popovers on rgba(30, 26, 22, 0.98), a "Delete?" danger fill with Cancel for confirmations, rows that open to a four-column figure block.
  • Main window: opaque canvas #141210, sidebar and title bar #0e0c0b, groups #1b1815 with 10 px radius; one cream primary button (#f3ebe1 on #17120d) per view; cream switches and sliders.
  • Reminder card: the meter's card at the meter's width (220 px over the capsule), 6 px above it; bell header, 13 px event names, 11.5 px facts, a 2 px time-left line; 150 ms fades, 30 s, paused under the pointer.
  • To-do window: the overlay card at 370 px; 26 px rows 2 px apart with a 12 px gutter so names line up under the section labels; done is a 1 px line through the name in muted; a 20 px check and × at the row's end under the pointer only; counts as a 46 px figure between two 20 px arrows. From the game: a 20 px source line under the selector, the figure without arrows in the stepper's 88 px with the extra entries in a 26 px slot, Lucide pencil in the gutter once typed over. The amount rows (a full one near its refill on rgba(242, 198, 109, 0.12); further away, "Full · refills …" in muted, no fill) are drawn in the Resources window only.
  • Fight report: the overlay card at 520 px (the detail window's); one player table with 54/46/52/50/40 px figure columns 6 px apart, the sorted one text 13.5 / 600, the others muted 12.5; 24 px death rows on track; figures left on plates and right in tables; "--" in faint for what was not recorded. On the summary card a 24 px full-width soft button; in History a 12 px Lucide crown in hp and a 20 px report button on boss records.
  • Hub: the toolbar is an overlay card at the user's opacity, 4 px padding, 12 px radius; 26 × 26 px tools with 14 px icons, lit = hot and a 4 px dot; 1 px rules at 0.18 between groups; counts "245/840 +380" on 26 px buttons, full near the refill = full-fill and warn; dropdowns are the popover, 268 px. The Resources window is the to-do window's frame with the amount rows, no × slot. The first-open prompt is a 310 px card placed as the reminder card. The meter's "…" menu is the 240 px popover with three items.
  • Updates: the toolbar's update icon is a 26 px tool, soft and text when an update waits, muted with a 2 px inset progress line while downloading, good on chip-good when ready; no new hue. What's new is the overlay card at 370 px (History's frame), versions 13.5 / 600, summary 12.5 / 500, bullets 12 / 500 muted. In Settings › About the download's progress is the Slider's 4 px track, and GitHub errors are field messages in warn.
