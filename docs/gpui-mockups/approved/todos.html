To-do window approved 2026-10-06 (direction E)

  • Its own overlay window for what resets and is lost if not done: Today (lost at the daily reset), Recharges (bank up to a cap, then wasted) and This week (lost on Wednesday). Same card, opacity, content scale and Barlow face as History, PvP and Detail; 370 px wide; one list per character, the selector following the character in game. Opened from "To-dos" in the meter's "…" menu.
  • The meter cannot read any of these counts from the game, so everything is set by hand: ticks (done or not), counts (X/N with one-less and one-more arrows; a click on the figure adds one) and optional amounts (empty and ignored until you type what you have; then the meter adds the refill and says when it is full, yellow once full).
  • A done to-do is struck through and muted where it stands; rows never move. A click anywhere on a tick row marks it done; under the pointer a small check ("Mark done", an undo arrow "Undo" on a done row) appears at its end. Counts are done at N, with no check.
  • No edit mode: a × at the row's end under the pointer hides it; each section ends with "N hidden · Show"; "+ Add a to-do" ends the list. The reset hour, the membership and bringing the defaults back are in Settings, To-dos.
  • Limits and refill rules are from the reset research (MetaBot's client tables for the Global client 0.0.4387.0; Game8 for Duty Missions and Duty Commands). Counts, times and character names are invented. Clock: Monday 21:48 (the fresh week: Wednesday 09:12). The last state is a working window; nothing is saved.

Behaviour

  • Window. An overlay window of its own like History and PvP: transparent, tool window, never activates, except while you type in a field (an amount, the new to-do's name), as the PvP name field does; Enter, Esc or a click outside gives the keyboard back to the game. Drag by the header; × closes it. Opened from "To-dos" in the meter's "…" menu, between PvP meter and Settings, with "1 full" beside it while an amount you typed is full.
  • Characters. One list per character the meter remembers; the selector follows the character in game ("Playing") and lists the others with what each has left. Duty Missions are per account and server ("Account" tag): one count shared by your characters on that server.
  • Sections. Today, Recharges and This week fold by a click on their label. Today and This week say how many are left ("done" in good) and when they reset; Recharges says how many amounts are tracked, "N full" and the next one to fill ("amounts optional" while none is typed). The title counts what is left in Today and This week, plus "N full".
  • Tick rows. The whole row is one button: a click anywhere marks it done or not done. Pointer on the row: the row lights (soft) and the check appears at its end, before the ×; the pointer on the check shows "Mark done" (on a done row, the undo arrow, "Undo"). No confirmation: a slip is one more click.
  • Count rows. One-less and one-more arrows around the figure; a click on the figure adds one; Up and Down on the focused figure. They stop at 0 and at N; at N the row is done (struck through, figure muted), the up arrow dims and the down arrow takes one back. No check on counts. Back to 0 at the daily reset (Today) or on Wednesday (This week).
  • Amounts. Empty by default: a quiet "—", left out of every count and warning. A click on it (or Enter) opens a field: Enter keeps, Esc cancels, an empty field or its × stops tracking. Once typed, the meter adds the refill on schedule, says "Full in 2 d 16 h", and at the cap turns the row yellow with the warning in the gutter: "Full, being wasted" (Odyle Energy, which refills every 3 h) or "Full, spend by Tue 16:00" (daily refills: the next refill is the one lost). After spending, correct it with the arrows (Odyle Energy 10 at a time, everything else 1) or by retyping.
  • Groups. Sanctuary and Exploration fold into one row each ("2 of 6 done"; all done: struck through, the figure in good); a click opens them in place.
  • Hiding. The × shows only while the pointer is on that row: it appears when the pointer enters the row and goes when it leaves, never on movement inside, so it cannot flash; its 20 px slot is kept on every row, so nothing shifts. A click hides the row (it stops counting anywhere). Your own to-dos are removed instead, with "Removed Legion check-in. Undo" for 4 s. After a hide or removal, the row that slides under the pointer keeps its × back until the pointer moves, so a double click hides one row.
  • Getting them back. Each section (and an open group) ends with "N hidden · Show" in faint; Show lists the hidden rows in place, faded (0.42), each with a put-back button in the × slot; the line then reads "Hide again". Duty Commands start hidden. Settings, To-dos brings every hidden one back at once.
  • Adding. "+ Add a to-do" ends the list; a click turns it into a form in place: the name field (focused), Add and Cancel; Tick or Count of N (2 to 99, 3 first) and Daily or Weekly. Enter adds, Esc or Cancel closes; a click outside closes it while the name is empty. The new to-do goes to the end of Today or This week (unfolding it), tagged "Yours", and "Added … to Today." shows for 4 s.
  • Footer. "Daily reset 09:00 · Change" (Change opens Settings, To-dos) and "Weekly on Wednesday". The hour is a setting: 09:00 is reported, not confirmed.
  • Keyboard and screen readers. Tab goes through the controls in reading order: the section labels, a tick or group row (one stop), a count's arrows and figure, an amount's figure, Show, "+ Add a to-do"; disabled arrows are skipped. A tick row is a button named with its state, "Legion check-in, not done"; Space or Enter toggles it and the window says "Legion check-in: done". A figure is a spin button, "Duty Missions, 4 of 5", then "5 of 5, done": Up and Down change it, Space or Enter adds one (on an amount, opens the field). Delete on any focused control of a row hides the row (removes your own) and focus moves to the next row. The check and the × are not tab stops but keep their names ("Mark done: Legion check-in", "Hide Nightmare"); they also show while the row has keyboard focus.

GPUI notes
