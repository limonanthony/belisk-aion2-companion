Behaviour

  • Window. An overlay window of its own like History and PvP: transparent, tool window, never activates, except while you type in a field (a value typed over the game's, the new to-do's name), as the PvP name field does; Enter, Esc or a click outside gives the keyboard back to the game. Drag by the header; × closes it. Opened and closed from the toolbar's checklist, which is lit while the window is open.
  • Characters. One list per character the meter remembers; the selector follows the character in game ("Playing") and lists the others with what each has left (to-dos only). Duty Missions are per account and server (the Account icon, Lucide users, after the name; the word "Account" is its tooltip, Pointer on the Account icon): one count shared by your characters on that server.
  • Where the values come from: one line under the selector, the meter's hint-line style. "From the game, live · logged in at 19:02"; between sessions "From the game at login, Sun 22:40" (nothing here refills between logins, so no estimate); before any login "No game values yet. Log in with Sefa while Belisk runs.". The figure's tooltip repeats it for that row ("2/3 at your login Sun 22:40.").
  • Game values have no arrows. The figure sits where a manual figure sits, the extra entries after it in the arrows' width, so nothing else in the row moves. Arrows stay on what you count yourself: Duty Missions, Duty Commands, your own to-dos, and Ascension Trial before the game has filled it.
  • Typing over a game value. A click on the figure (Enter on the keyboard) opens the field with the done count in it. Enter keeps your number (0 to the target); Esc cancels. Your number takes the figure's place with a pencil in the gutter ("Typed by you. The game said 2."); the extra entries stay the game's. The game's next value (a run, an entry from an item, your next login) replaces it. In the field, × or an empty field goes back to the game's value at once.
  • At login the game's snapshot fills every row it covers and replaces what you typed there; a good note says so for 4 s ("Filled from the game at login. Values you typed were replaced.").
  • Between sessions the window keeps each character's last game values. Runs are never guessed. A weekly row seen before Wednesday's reset starts over at the reset.
  • Weekly rows. The game sends the entries left; the row reads done of target, done = target − left (Ascension Trial 1 left of 3 reads 2/3), done (struck through) at 0 left; extra entries beside it ("+1"), which do not stop a row from being done.
  • Sections. Today and This week fold by a click on their label, say how many are left ("done" in good) and when they reset. The title counts what is left in both; nothing about resources.
  • Tick rows. The whole row is one button: a click anywhere marks it done or not done. Pointer on the row: the row lights (soft) and the check appears at its end, before the ×; the pointer on the check shows "Mark done" (on a done row, the undo arrow, "Undo"). No confirmation: a slip is one more click.
  • Count rows you keep yourself. One-less and one-more arrows around the figure; a click on the figure adds one; Up and Down on the focused figure. They stop at 0 and at N; at N the row is done (struck through, figure muted), the up arrow dims and the down arrow takes one back. No check on counts. Back to 0 at the daily reset (Today) or on Wednesday (This week).
  • Resources are not here. Amounts (Odyle Energy, Shugo keys, Invasion keys, Nightmare, the Daily Dungeon) and their full warning are the Resources window's. A recharge row hidden here before 2026-10-08 shows there again: that window has nothing to hide.
  • Hiding. The × shows only while the pointer is on that row: it appears when the pointer enters the row and goes when it leaves, never on movement inside, so it cannot flash; its 20 px slot is kept on every row, so nothing shifts. A click hides the row (it stops counting anywhere). Your own to-dos are removed instead, with "Removed Legion check-in. Undo" for 4 s. After a hide or removal, the row that slides under the pointer keeps its × back until the pointer moves, so a double click hides one row.
  • Getting them back. Each section ends with "N hidden · Show" in faint; Show lists the hidden rows in place, faded (0.42), each with a put-back button in the × slot; the line then reads "Hide again". Duty Commands start hidden. Settings, To-dos brings every hidden one back at once.
  • Adding. "+ Add a to-do" ends the list; a click turns it into a form in place: the name field (focused), Add and Cancel; Tick or Count of N (2 to 99, 3 first) and Daily or Weekly. Enter adds, Esc or Cancel closes; a click outside closes it while the name is empty. The new to-do goes to the end of Today or This week (unfolding it), tagged "Yours", and "Added … to Today." shows for 4 s.
  • Footer. "Daily reset 09:00 · Change" (Change opens Settings, To-dos) and "Weekly on Wednesday". The hour is a setting: 09:00 is reported, not confirmed; it also sets the Resources window's daily refills.
  • Keyboard and screen readers. Tab goes through the controls in reading order: the section labels, a tick row (one stop), a count's arrows and figure, a game figure, Show, "+ Add a to-do"; disabled arrows are skipped. A tick row is a button named with its state, "Legion check-in, not done"; Space or Enter toggles it and the window says "Legion check-in: done". A figure you count is a spin button, "Duty Missions, 4 of 5", then "5 of 5, done": Up and Down change it, Space or Enter adds one. A game figure is a button, "Ascension Trial, 2 of 3 done, 1 extra, from the game. Type over it": Enter opens the field. Delete on any focused control of a row hides the row (removes your own) and focus moves to the next row. The check and the × are not tab stops but keep their names ("Mark done: Legion check-in", "Hide Duty Missions"); they also show while the row has keyboard focus.
