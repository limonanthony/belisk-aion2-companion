Behaviour

  • Window. An overlay window of its own like History and PvP: transparent, tool window, never activates, except while you type in a field (an amount, a value typed over the game's, the new to-do's name), as the PvP name field does; Enter, Esc or a click outside gives the keyboard back to the game. Drag by the header; × closes it. Opened and closed from the toolbar's checklist, which is lit while the window is open.
  • Characters. One list per character the meter remembers; the selector follows the character in game ("Playing") and lists the others with what each has left. Duty Missions, Shugo Festival keys, Invasion keys and the Unknown Fissure are per account and server ("Account" tag): one value shared by your characters on that server, and a login with any of them fills the game's for all of them.
  • Where the values come from: one line under the selector, the meter's hint-line style. "From the game, live · logged in at 19:02"; between sessions "From the game at login, Sun 22:40 · refills since estimated"; before any login "No game values yet. Log in with Sefa while Kisk runs."; a new character on a server already seen "Tavrin not seen at login yet · Account rows from Mirelle". The figure's tooltip repeats it for that row ("125 at your login Sun 22:40. +120 since: eight refills of 15, estimated.").
  • Two pools. A recharge shows its regular pool against the cap ("7/21") and, when it is not zero, the extra pool beside it ("+3"). A total above the cap is normal: no error, no colour. "Full, being wasted" (or "Full, spend by …") lights only when the regular pool reaches its cap; extras never trigger it, and "Full in …" counts only the regular pool. Membership (Settings, To-dos) picks the membership cap of every ticket: Shugo Festival keys 12 → 21, Odyle Energy 560 → 840.
  • Game values have no arrows. The figure sits where a manual figure sits, the extra pool after it in the arrows' width, so nothing else in the row moves. Arrows stay on what you count yourself: Duty Missions, Duty Commands, your own to-dos, and every row before the game has filled it.
  • Typing over a game value. A click on the figure (Enter on the keyboard) opens the field with the regular pool in it. Enter keeps your number, above the cap too; Esc cancels. Your number takes the figure's place with a pencil in the gutter ("Typed by you. The game said 7."); the extra pool stays the game's. The game's next value for that ticket (a spend, a refill, an item, your next login) replaces it. In the field, × or an empty field goes back to the game's value at once.
  • At login the game's snapshot fills every row it covers and replaces what you typed there; a good note says so for 4 s ("Filled from the game at login. Values you typed were replaced.").
  • Between sessions the window keeps each character's last game values and adds the refills the table knows since (daily +N at the reset, Odyle +15 every 3 h), up to the cap. Spends are never guessed. A weekly row seen before Wednesday's reset starts over at the reset.
  • Weekly rows. The game sends the entries left; the row reads done of target, done = target − left (Unknown Fissure 9 left of 14 reads 5/14), done (struck through) at 0 left; extra entries beside it ("+7"), which do not stop a row from being done. A final-boss tick (Abyssal Forge) is done when its ticket reaches 0.
  • Membership off, the game shows more. A regular pool above the cap in force (18 Shugo keys with the switch off) can only come from a membership: the row shows it as it is, never full and without an estimate, and Settings, To-dos asks about the membership. Membership off again cuts nothing.
  • Sections. Today, Recharges and This week fold by a click on their label. Today and This week say how many are left ("done" in good) and when they reset; Recharges says how many amounts are tracked, "N full" and the next one to fill ("amounts optional" while none has a value). The title counts what is left in Today and This week, plus "N full".
  • Tick rows. The whole row is one button: a click anywhere marks it done or not done. Pointer on the row: the row lights (soft) and the check appears at its end, before the ×; the pointer on the check shows "Mark done" (on a done row, the undo arrow, "Undo"). No confirmation: a slip is one more click.
  • Count rows you keep yourself. One-less and one-more arrows around the figure; a click on the figure adds one; Up and Down on the focused figure. They stop at 0 and at N; at N the row is done (struck through, figure muted), the up arrow dims and the down arrow takes one back. No check on counts. Back to 0 at the daily reset (Today) or on Wednesday (This week).
  • Amounts before the game fills them. Empty by default: a quiet "—", left out of every count and warning. A click on it (or Enter) opens a field: Enter keeps (above the cap too), Esc cancels, an empty field or its × stops tracking. A typed number is your total: at or above the cap the row counts as full. Once typed, the meter adds the refill on schedule, says "Full in 2 d 16 h", and at the cap turns the row yellow with the warning in the gutter: "Full, being wasted" (Odyle Energy, which refills every 3 h) or "Full, spend by Tue 09:00" (daily refills: the next refill is the one lost). After spending, correct it with the arrows (Odyle Energy 10 at a time, everything else 1) or by retyping. Once the game fills the row, its values take over.
  • Groups. Sanctuary, Exploration and Conquest dungeons fold into one row each ("2 of 6 done"; all done: struck through, the figure in good; Conquest dungeons: "10–13 of 14 each", or "1 full" in warn); a click opens them in place.
  • Hiding. The × shows only while the pointer is on that row: it appears when the pointer enters the row and goes when it leaves, never on movement inside, so it cannot flash; its 20 px slot is kept on every row, so nothing shifts. A click hides the row (it stops counting anywhere). Your own to-dos are removed instead, with "Removed Legion check-in. Undo" for 4 s. After a hide or removal, the row that slides under the pointer keeps its × back until the pointer moves, so a double click hides one row.
  • Getting them back. Each section (and an open group) ends with "N hidden · Show" in faint; Show lists the hidden rows in place, faded (0.42), each with a put-back button in the × slot; the line then reads "Hide again". Duty Commands start hidden. Settings, To-dos brings every hidden one back at once.
  • Adding. "+ Add a to-do" ends the list; a click turns it into a form in place: the name field (focused), Add and Cancel; Tick or Count of N (2 to 99, 3 first) and Daily or Weekly. Enter adds, Esc or Cancel closes; a click outside closes it while the name is empty. The new to-do goes to the end of Today or This week (unfolding it), tagged "Yours", and "Added … to Today." shows for 4 s.
  • Footer. "Daily reset 09:00 · Change" (Change opens Settings, To-dos) and "Weekly on Wednesday". The hour is a setting: 09:00 is reported, not confirmed. Shugo Festival keys (+3) and Invasion keys (+1) refill at the daily reset, as the ticket table's daily clock says (16:00 KST = 09:00 here).
  • Keyboard and screen readers. Tab goes through the controls in reading order: the section labels, a tick or group row (one stop), a count's arrows and figure, an amount's figure, a game figure, Show, "+ Add a to-do"; disabled arrows are skipped. A tick row is a button named with its state, "Legion check-in, not done"; Space or Enter toggles it and the window says "Legion check-in: done". A figure you count is a spin button, "Duty Missions, 4 of 5", then "5 of 5, done": Up and Down change it, Space or Enter adds one (on an amount, opens the field). A game figure is a button, "Daily Dungeon, Unknown Fissure, 5 of 14 done, from the game. Type over it": Enter opens the field. Delete on any focused control of a row hides the row (removes your own) and focus moves to the next row. The check and the × are not tab stops but keep their names ("Mark done: Legion check-in", "Hide Nightmare"); they also show while the row has keyboard focus.
