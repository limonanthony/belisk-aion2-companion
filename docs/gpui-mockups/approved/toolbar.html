Toolbar approved 2026-10-08 (the hub)

  • Kisk is a set of tools over the game, not only a DPS meter. The toolbar is its hub: a small horizontal bar floating over the game. At launch only the toolbar opens; every tool opens and closes from it. Like the reminder card it never takes the keyboard from the game (no-activate, tool window), and it is never click-through: Alt+CapsLock locks the meter only.
  • Left to right: the grip; DPS meter (crossed swords), PvP meter (crosshair), To-dos (checklist), Fight history (crown: crown = history and boss records, skull = deaths), Reminders (bell), Settings (gear); Odyle Energy and Shugo keys; the warning while other resources are full; fold and quit. A lit tool (hot fill and a dot) is open; a click opens or closes it.
  • Decided with the user in the hub round (hub/README.md, the record of how it was chosen). Values are in tokens.md, "Hub". Values and times are invented; the clock is Monday 21:48. Conquest dungeons, Deus Research Base, Shattered Arkanis, Abandoned Balaur Fortress and Fafnium Laboratory have no count and are never in the warning (settled in game, 2026-10-08: only Odyle Energy limits the first three on the Global servers; the Daily Dungeon menu lists only the Unknown Fissure).

Behaviour

  • Horizontal only, because of the counts. At launch only the toolbar opens; the meter opens from its icon, or at launch with Settings › Overlay › "Open the meter at launch" (off by default). The first time the meter is opened from the toolbar, the first-open prompt asks about that setting.
  • Counts: icons only. Odyle Energy and Shugo keys are always shown as the Resources window draws them: the regular pool against its cap ("245/840"), the extra pool beside it when not zero ("+380"); above the cap is normal. Amber only when the regular pool is full and the refill it would lose is near: within 1 h of Odyle's 3-hour refill, 3 h of a daily one (Shugo keys). Full but further away, the count looks normal and the tooltip says "Full · refills Tue 09:00". A click on either opens the Resources window; while it is open the counts read lit, and a click on a count closes it again. The tooltips name them as the Resources rows do, "Odyle Energy" and "Shugo keys" (2026-10-08: "yeah shugo keys is ok"). Since 2026-10-08 the to-do window has no resources ("I think we can remove the recharges from the todos now no ?"): the toolbar and the Resources window are where they show.
  • The warning (Lucide triangle-alert and the number) shows only while other resources are full and their refill is near (3 h for Invasion keys and Nightmare, 24 h for the Daily Dungeon's weekly one). A click opens its dropdown: "Full, a refill is coming", then each one with its figure and refill time right-aligned in columns ("Invasion keys 7/7 · refills 09:00"), a divider, "All resources". A click on a row or on "All resources" opens the Resources window. A resource hidden earlier in the to-do window counts too: nothing hides a resource any more.
  • Before any game value: a faint dash in place of the figure; the tooltip says "No value yet. Log in while Kisk runs."
  • Reminders: the bell opens a small dropdown: the next three reminders switched on, "Pause reminders" (the Settings master switch; while paused the bell turns to Lucide bell-off in faint) and "Reminder settings" (Settings › Reminders).
  • The PvP meter takes Lucide crosshair; the crossed swords are the DPS meter's. The PvP window's header changes to match (pvp.html).
  • Focus: the toolbar, its tooltips and dropdowns never take the keyboard. Its dropdowns are drawn inside its window, which grows downward while one is open (upward when the toolbar sits low on the screen), as the meter's "…" menu does. A dropdown closes on a click elsewhere in the toolbar, a second click on its button, or the pointer leaving the toolbar and the dropdown.
  • Pointer: tools and counts light on pointer enter and go on leave, never on moves inside; tooltips after 500 ms, as the meter's. The meter's tooltip says what closing it means: "Fights are tracked while it is open." (open: "Click to close. Fights stop being tracked.").
  • What is tracked when: capture always runs. Fights (damage, heals, damage taken, deaths, fight history) are tracked only while the meter is open; resources always stay up to date when the game sends them. The to-dos work without the meter.
  • Place: the toolbar opens where you left it (top centre the first time), dragged by the grip or any empty part of the bar. Tools keep their own places.
  • Fold (« at the end) leaves a small handle: the grip, unfold (») and, when anything is full near its refill (Odyle and Shugo keys included), the warning with that number.
  • Quit: × after fold quits Kisk at once, with the tooltip "Quit Kisk / Closes every tool"; no confirmation. Closing the toolbar ends the process.
  • Hide everything: Alt+Shift+CapsLock hides the toolbar and every open tool, and shows them again; the tray icon's "Show toolbar" too. Alt+CapsLock keeps locking the meter.
  • The reminder card sits above the meter while the meter is open; while it is closed, 6 px under the toolbar, left edges aligned (above it when the toolbar is low on the screen).
  • Name: "Kisk" where the toolbar names the app (the quit tooltip). The app's other names (title bars, About, the waiting card's "DPS Meter") change with the rename (TASK-23).
  • French: drawn to check the room; the real text goes through i18n.
