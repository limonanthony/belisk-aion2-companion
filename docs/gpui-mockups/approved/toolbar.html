Toolbar approved 2026-10-08 (the hub); the update icon 2026-10-09; the meter stepping aside between fights 2026-10-09

  • Belisk is a set of tools over the game, not only a DPS meter. The toolbar is its hub: a small horizontal bar floating over the game. At launch only the toolbar opens; every tool opens and closes from it. Like the reminder card it never takes the keyboard from the game (no-activate, tool window), and it is never click-through: Alt+CapsLock locks the meter only.
  • Left to right: the grip; DPS meter (crossed swords), PvP meter (crosshair), To-dos (checklist), Fight history (crown: crown = history and boss records, skull = deaths), Reminders (bell), Settings (gear); Odyle Energy and Shugo keys; the warning while other resources are full; fold and quit. A lit tool (hot fill and a dot) is open; a click opens or closes it.
  • Decided with the user in the hub round (hub/README.md, the record of how it was chosen). Values are in tokens.md, "Hub". Values and times are invented; the clock is Monday 21:48. Conquest dungeons, Deus Research Base, Shattered Arkanis, Abandoned Balaur Fortress and Fafnium Laboratory have no count and are never in the warning (settled in game, 2026-10-08: only Odyle Energy limits the first three on the Global servers; the Daily Dungeon menu lists only the Unknown Fissure).
  • Added 2026-10-09 (the updates round, updates/README.md): the update icon, first in the end group before fold, shown only while there is something to do or know: an update to download, a download running, an update ready to install. Settings › About has the rest (About › Updates), and What's new opens once after the restart. States "Update available" to "Try the update".

Behaviour

  • Horizontal only, because of the counts. At launch only the toolbar opens; the meter opens from its icon, or at launch with Settings › Overlay › "Open the meter at launch" (off by default). The first time the meter is opened from the toolbar, the first-open prompt asks about that setting.
  • Counts: icons only. Odyle Energy and Shugo keys are always shown as the Resources window draws them: the regular pool against its cap ("245/840"), the extra pool beside it when not zero ("+380"); above the cap is normal. Amber only when the regular pool is full and the refill it would lose is near: within 1 h of Odyle's 3-hour refill, 3 h of a daily one (Shugo keys). Full but further away, the count looks normal and the tooltip says "Full · refills Tue 09:00". A click on either opens the Resources window; while it is open the counts read lit, and a click on a count closes it again. The tooltips name them as the Resources rows do, "Odyle Energy" and "Shugo keys" (2026-10-08: "yeah shugo keys is ok"). Since 2026-10-08 the to-do window has no resources ("I think we can remove the recharges from the todos now no ?"): the toolbar and the Resources window are where they show.
  • The warning (Lucide triangle-alert and the number) shows only while other resources are full and their refill is near (3 h for Invasion keys and Nightmare, 24 h for the Daily Dungeon's weekly one). A click opens its dropdown: "Full, a refill is coming", then each one with its figure and refill time right-aligned in columns ("Invasion keys 7/7 · refills 09:00"), a divider, "All resources". A click on a row or on "All resources" opens the Resources window. A resource hidden earlier in the to-do window counts too: nothing hides a resource any more.
  • Before any game value: a faint dash in place of the figure; the tooltip says "No value yet. Log in while Belisk runs."
  • Reminders: the bell opens a small dropdown: the next three reminders switched on, "Pause reminders" (the Settings master switch; while paused the bell turns to Lucide bell-off in faint) and "Reminder settings" (Settings › Reminders).
  • The PvP meter takes Lucide crosshair; the crossed swords are the DPS meter's. The PvP window's header changes to match (pvp.html).
  • Focus: the toolbar, its tooltips and dropdowns never take the keyboard. Its dropdowns are drawn inside its window, which grows downward while one is open (upward when the toolbar sits low on the screen), as the meter's "…" menu does. A dropdown closes on a click elsewhere in the toolbar, a second click on its button, or the pointer leaving the toolbar and the dropdown.
  • Pointer: tools and counts light on pointer enter and go on leave, never on moves inside; tooltips after 500 ms, as the meter's. The meter's tooltip says what closing it means: "Fights are tracked while it is open." (open: "Click to close. Fights stop being tracked."). With Settings › Overlay › "Hide between fights" on (added 2026-10-09, off by default), an open meter steps aside while you are not fighting and comes back at your first hit; it is still open (lit, fights tracked), its tooltip says "Hidden between fights. Click to show it for 10 s.", and a click shows it for 10 s instead of closing it.
  • What is tracked when: capture always runs. Fights (damage, heals, damage taken, deaths, fight history) are tracked only while the meter is open; resources always stay up to date when the game sends them. The to-dos work without the meter.
  • Place: the toolbar opens where you left it (top centre the first time), dragged by the grip or any empty part of the bar. Tools keep their own places.
  • Fold (« at the end) leaves a small handle: the grip, unfold (») and, when anything is full near its refill (Odyle and Shugo keys included), the warning with that number.
  • Quit: × after fold quits the app at once, with the tooltip "Quit Belisk / Closes every tool"; no confirmation. Closing the toolbar ends the process.
  • Hide everything: Alt+Shift+CapsLock hides the toolbar and every open tool, and shows them again; the tray icon's "Show toolbar" too. Alt+CapsLock keeps locking the meter.
  • The reminder card sits above the meter while the meter is open; while it is closed, 6 px under the toolbar, left edges aligned (above it when the toolbar is low on the screen).
  • Name: the toolbar names the app (the quit tooltip, its accessible name, "Log in while … runs.") with the Fluent term -app-name, as the build's toolbar-quit = Quit { -app-name }; here it comes from one constant, app-name.js, so a rename changes one place.
  • French: drawn to check the room; the real text goes through i18n.

The update icon (approved 2026-10-09)

  • When it shows: available, downloading, paused, checking the download, ready, and after a failed download. Up to date, checking for updates, GitHub unreachable or limiting: no icon (Settings › About only). Checks at launch, then once an hour while the app runs, never more often (GitHub's 60 requests an hour per address); there is no switch to turn them off. The hourly check goes on while an update is available or ready, so a newer release replaces a staged one.
  • Place: first in the end group, before fold (… warning | update « ×): it is about the app, like fold and quit, and the tools and counts keep their places when it comes and goes. A 26 px tool, not a 20 px end button, because it carries an action. Folded, the handle keeps it after the warning.
  • Looks, no new hue: available, Lucide download in text on the soft fill (a tool at rest has no fill, so it reads as "something waits"); downloading, the same icon in muted with a 2 px progress line inset at the bottom (the reminder card's time-left line); paused, the line in faint; checking the download, the line full; ready, Lucide rotate-cw in good on chip-good. A failed download (its hash or ed25519 signature did not match: deleted, nothing installed) shows the available look again and says so in its tooltip; no colour, no pop-up during play.
  • Clicks: available, the download starts at once (no confirmation); downloading, paused or checking, opens Settings › About, where Cancel and the details are; ready, restarts at once, no confirmation, as quit has none. During a fight Restart still works, and the tooltip warns that it splits the fight in History.
  • Download: "Download updates automatically" (Settings › About) is on by default; the download is always capped at 1 MB/s and paused while you fight, also when you clicked Update yourself. With it on, a failed download is tried again at the next hourly check, never in a loop. Installing always waits for a click on Restart.
  • After the restart: the toolbar comes back where it was, and the tools that were open open again in their places (the meter too, so fights keep being tracked); then What's new opens once under it.
  • Pointer: lights on enter and goes on leave, never on moves inside; the tooltip after 500 ms; the progress line changes only when the download progresses (one frame per percent); nothing pulses.
  • Accessibility: the button's name is the tooltip's first line ("Update available: 1.6.0", "Downloading 1.6.0 · 62%", "Restart to update to 1.6.0"); the line is a progressbar with its value; Left and Right reach it like the other tools.
  • Versions and times are invented: the build runs 1.5.1, GitHub's latest is 1.6.0 (the version resets to 1.0.0 first).
