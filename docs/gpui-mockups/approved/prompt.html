First-open prompt approved 2026-10-08 (the hub)

  • Belisk starts with the toolbar alone. The first time you open the meter from the toolbar, a small card asks whether the meter should open at every launch, and says where that setting lives: Settings › Overlay › "Open the meter at launch" (off by default). Two buttons; never asked again once answered.
  • The card is drawn like the reminder card: its own small overlay window, 6 px above the meter, left edges aligned, the meter's width. Values are in tokens.md, "Hub".

Behaviour

  • Asked once, the first time the meter is opened from the toolbar, with the path to the setting. Off by default: without an answer the meter does not open at launch.
  • Yes, at launch turns the setting on; the card shows a good note with the path for 4 s, then fades out (150 ms, as the reminder card). Not now closes it at once and leaves the setting off. Either answer is final: the card never comes back.
  • No answer: the card stays while the meter is open (no time-out); closing the meter without answering asks again the next time.
  • Window: no-activate like the reminder card (the game keeps the keyboard), clickable even while the meter is locked. Placement as the reminder card: above the meter, below it when there is no room; 220 px over the capsule. A reminder that comes while the card is up stacks 6 px above it.
