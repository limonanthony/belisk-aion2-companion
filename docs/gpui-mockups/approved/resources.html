Resources window approved 2026-10-08 (the hub)

  • Every resource the game fills, for the current character, in a floating window of its own: opened by a click on Odyle Energy or Shugo keys in the toolbar, or on "All resources" at the end of the warning's dropdown. The to-do window keeps its Recharges section too.
  • Built from the to-do window: the same frame, character selector, source line, rows and display rules, so both windows always agree. Values are in tokens.md, "Hub" (Resources window) and "To-do window".
  • Dashed: to confirm in game, drawn with the build's default (the Deus Research Base and Shattered Arkanis cap of 4; Conquest dungeons as one ticket per dungeon). Values and times are invented; the clock is Monday 21:48, Mirelle logged in at 19:02, membership on.

Behaviour

  • Rows: the to-do window's Recharges rows, in the same order. Weekly entries (Unknown Fissure, Sanctuary…) stay to-dos: they are done or not, not a pool that refills.
  • Display: the regular pool against its cap ("7/21"), the extra pool beside it when not zero ("+3"); above the cap is normal, never an error; "Full, spend by …" / "Full, being wasted" only when the regular pool is at its cap and a refill is coming. Membership (Settings, To-dos) picks the membership caps (Shugo keys 12 → 21, Odyle Energy 560 → 840). Shugo keys and Invasion keys are shared by the characters of a server ("Account").
  • Always up to date: resources follow the game's messages whether the meter is open or not.
  • Same data as the to-dos: typing over a value, the pencil, the tooltips and "from yesterday's login" work here as in the to-do window, and a change in one window shows in the other.
  • Frame: the to-do window's 370 px, a header with Lucide package and "Resources", the "2 full" chip, the character selector (follows the character in game; others can be looked at), the source line; no × slot on rows (nothing to hide). Footer: the daily reset, and which caps are in force ("Membership caps").
  • Window: an overlay window like the to-do window (no-activate, except while a field takes the keyboard); opens where you left it, one at a time; the toolbar's counts read lit while it is open, and a click on a count closes it again.
  • To confirm in game: the Deus Research Base and Shattered Arkanis cap (drawn: 4, the ticket table) and whether a Conquest entry spends its dungeon's ticket (drawn: six rows of 14, +2 a day) or a shared pool.
