Behaviour

  • Rows: Odyle Energy, Shugo keys, Invasion keys, Nightmare, Daily Dungeon, in that order. The Daily Dungeon (ticket 101) is tickets left of 14 with the extra entries beside it ("9/14 +7"), +14 at the weekly reset on Wednesday; it has no dungeon name, because its menu lists one dungeon ("I think you don't need to put the dungeon name in the todo just put daily dungeon with the tickets and it's ok", 2026-10-08). Shugo keys, Invasion keys and the Daily Dungeon carry the Account icon (Lucide users) without the word; the word shows under the pointer, so a warned row keeps its whole name ("yeah shugo keys is ok", 2026-10-08). Weekly entries (Ascension Trial) stay to-dos: they are done or not, not a pool that refills. Conquest dungeons, Deus Research Base and Shattered Arkanis have no row: on the Global servers only Odyle Energy limits them. Abandoned Balaur Fortress and Fafnium Laboratory have no row: the Daily Dungeon menu lists only the Unknown Fissure. Both settled in game, 2026-10-08.
  • Nothing to hide: no × slot on rows, so names get 26 px more. A row hidden earlier in the to-do window (Nightmare, say) shows here like the others and counts in "N full" and in the toolbar's warning.
  • Two pools: the regular pool against its cap ("7/21"), the extra pool beside it when not zero ("+3"). A total above the cap is normal: no error, no colour. Extras never make a row full, and "Full in …" counts only the regular pool. Settings, To-dos sets the caps (Membership: Shugo keys 12 → 21, Odyle Energy 560 → 840) and the refill times (Daily reset: Shugo keys +3, Invasion keys +1 and Nightmare +2 at that hour, the ticket table's daily clock, 16:00 KST = 09:00 here; the Daily Dungeon +14 on Wednesday at that hour; Odyle Energy +15 every 3 h).
  • Full warns only near the refill (2026-10-08: "if the main tickets are at 14/14 we put a warning but only when it's near the end of the possible usage if possible. Otherwise it's annoying to always have warnings even when not near the end (or near a replenishment for some currencies)"). A full regular pool turns the row yellow with the warning in the gutter, "Full, being wasted" (Odyle) or "Full, spend by Tue 09:00", and counts in "N full" (the header and the toolbar) only when the refill it would lose is near: within 1 h of Odyle's 3-hour refill, 3 h of a daily refill (Shugo keys, Invasion keys, Nightmare), 24 h of the weekly one (Daily Dungeon). Further away the row looks normal: the figure at its cap and "Full · refills Wed 09:00" in the muted status style, no warning, not counted.
  • Where the values come from: one line under the selector. "From the game, live · logged in at 19:02"; between sessions "From the game at login, Sun 22:40 · refills since estimated"; before any login "No game values yet. Log in with Sefa while Belisk runs."; a new character on a server already seen "Tavrin not seen at login yet · Account rows from Mirelle" (a login with any character of the server fills Shugo keys, Invasion keys and the Daily Dungeon for all of them). The figure's tooltip repeats it for that row ("125 at your login Sun 22:40. +120 since: eight refills of 15, estimated.").
  • Game values have no arrows: the figure sits where a counted figure sits, the extra pool after it in the arrows' width, so nothing else in the row moves.
  • Typing over a game value: a click on the figure (Enter on the keyboard) opens the field with the regular pool in it. Enter keeps your number, above the cap too; Esc cancels. Your number takes the figure's place with a pencil in the gutter ("Typed by you. The game said 7."); the extra pool stays the game's. The game's next value for that ticket (a spend, a refill, an item, your next login) replaces it. In the field, × or an empty field goes back to the game's value at once.
  • At login the game's snapshot fills every row it covers and replaces what you typed there; a good note says so for 4 s ("Filled from the game at login. Values you typed were replaced.").
  • Between sessions the window keeps each character's last game values and adds the refills the table knows since (daily +N at the reset, Odyle +15 every 3 h), up to the cap. Spends are never guessed.
  • Amounts before the game fills them: empty by default, a quiet "—", left out of every count and warning. A click on it (or Enter) opens a field: Enter keeps (above the cap too), Esc cancels, an empty field or its × stops tracking. A typed number is your total: at or above the cap the row counts as full. Once typed, the meter adds the refill on schedule, says "Full in 2 d 16 h", and at the cap reads "Full · refills …", then, near the refill, turns the row yellow with the warning in the gutter. After spending, correct it with the arrows (Odyle Energy 10 at a time, everything else 1) or by retyping. Once the game fills the row, its values take over. Settings, To-dos › Start over empties what you typed here too.
  • Membership off, the game shows more: a regular pool above the cap in force (18 Shugo keys with the switch off) can only come from a membership: the row shows it as it is, never full and without an estimate, and Settings, To-dos asks about the membership. Membership off again cuts nothing.
  • Keyboard and screen readers: Tab goes through the figures in reading order. A game figure is a button, "Daily Dungeon, 9 of 14, 7 extra, from the game. Type over it": Enter opens the field. An amount you count is a spin button, "Odyle Energy, 425 of 840": Up and Down change it, Enter opens the field.
  • Always up to date: resources follow the game's messages whether the meter is open or not, and whether this window is open or not.
  • Frame: the to-do window's 370 px, a header with Lucide package and "Resources", the "2 full" chip, the character selector (follows the character in game; others can be looked at, with what each has left, and the note "Characters Belisk has seen, with what each has left. Shugo keys, Invasion keys and the Daily Dungeon are shared by your characters on one server."), the source line. Footer: the daily reset, and which caps are in force ("Membership caps").
  • Window: an overlay window like the to-do window (no-activate, except while a field takes the keyboard); opens where you left it, one at a time; the toolbar's counts read lit while it is open, and a click on a count closes it again.
